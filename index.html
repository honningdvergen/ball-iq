Football quizzes, Footle and today's puzzles

Ball IQ is a free football quiz website. Nothing to install and no account needed — find your club or league at the top of the page and play in your browser. Whether you call it football or soccer, the questions are the same.

Today

Four puzzles reset at midnight, the same for everyone. Footle is the football Wordle — guess the footballer's surname in six. The Daily 7 is seven questions in about three minutes. Transfer Trail names a player from his moves, and Mystery Player asks who it is from career clues.

Your club

Every club on file has its own quiz drawn from its own history — from Liverpool, Arsenal and Manchester United to Celtic, Galatasaray, Boca Juniors and Hajduk Split — plus league and tournament quizzes for the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, the Bundesliga, the Champions League and the World Cup. Browse clubs by league.

Every game

Classic ten-question rounds, Survival, Hot Streak, Legends, Stadiums, Guess the XI, Chaos, Club Quiz and League Quiz — and live rooms for up to eight players, or one phone passed around a table. All free, all in the browser. See the full quiz index.

Lists and records

Alongside the games there are reference lists — every Ballon d'Or winner, every Champions League final, league top scorers, World Cup winners and more — kept as plain, checkable tables for when you want the fact rather than the game.

How it works

Most answers come with a short explanation of why they are right — the season it happened, who else was involved, what made it matter. Ball IQ is independent, built by one developer, and free to play. About the project, or get in touch if a question looks wrong — corrections from players are how the bank stays accurate.
