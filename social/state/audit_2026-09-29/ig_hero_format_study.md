## Why the winners won [INFERRED from the 15 + data]
1. **The photo ARGUES with the tweet (irony) and the face carries the emotion.** Pedri kissing the World Cup under "trophyless Bellingham … 'World Cup winner' Pedri"; Drogba holding the PL trophy under "overrated"; Garnacho hands-on-head under "NOT included"; Bruno grimacing under "biggest underachiever"; Kane looking away under "no one can rob anyone". The pairing IS the original joke (Football Planet trick) — Meta's originality test. Losers have neutral photos (Villarreal duel, Gabriel laughing) or a photo unrelated to the tweet.
2. **The tweet is a hot take that starts an argument or attacks a big name**, 10–25 words, one tweet only. Winners: Ballon d'Or debate (5 of the top 8 landed on shortlist day 09-08/09), Bruno "underachiever", Drogba "overrated", United "favourites", Enzo salary jab, Liverpool physique shock. Losers: praise, stats, puns, niche in-jokes (Mainoo praise, Gabriel record, Villarreal yellow), or **two stacked tweets** (Isak, Janty) that need reading.
3. **Sends (DM shares) are the reach engine:** winners have 1–15% sends per view (Pedri 15%, Liverpool 12.6%, Bruno 6%, Garnacho 4.3%); losers 1–2%. The sendable ones are "tag your Real Madrid/United/Liverpool mate" posts. [DATA: across 270 feed posts, sends/reach barely predicts FOLLOWS (corr 0.12; likes/reach 0.26): sends buy reach, identity-match buys follows — the top-sends quartile converted 4.1 vs 3.6 follows/10K.]
4. **Mega-global subject on a mega day.** Bellingham/Pedri/Kane/Yamal/Bruno/Garnacho/Liverpool; Ballon d'Or list day 09-08, deadline day 09-03, CL day 09-09/11. Nothing niche.
5. **Production:** full-bleed high-res photo, subject centred, tweet card lower third (~30% of frame), 8–10 slides, popular audio (all winners; also most losers, so audio ≠ differentiator). Losers: letterboxed photo with black bars (Isak), short 4-slide (Gabriel, no audio), two tweets. Card colour (white, dark, translucent) doesn't matter.
6. **Caption:** plain-spoken, opinionated first line ("Not even on Lacazette's level? Typed that with his full chest 😭"); the first-slide identity matters more than the caption (one caption describes Olise under a Mbappé slide and still did 89.6K).

## Hero-slide spec (adopt as slide 1 of every carousel and as a daily single) — goes into A_GRADE_PLAN.md
- ONE tweet ≤ 25 words that is a hot take, an attack or a shock on a mega name (real fan/verified account, real handle). No two-tweet stacks, no praise/stats.
- A full-bleed high-res photo (no letterbox) where the face's expression and/or the trophy/kit context contradicts or confirms the tweet.
- Tweet card in the lower third; keep the same look (white default, dark variant ok).
- Post on the mega topic of the day (transfer/awards/verdict/match), within ~24 h.
- KPI: sends per view ≥5% and follows per 10K ≥3; kill or vary formats scoring under 2% sends.
- Daily: 1 hero single/carousel on IG, the same hero as an image on Threads/Facebook (FB video slideshow) and X as a caption-less picture joke if the photo carries it.
