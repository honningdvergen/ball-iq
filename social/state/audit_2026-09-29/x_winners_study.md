## What it says
1. **X winners are short-caption image jokes (3–8 words) built on a recognisable player/fanbase gag** — not news lines. 8 of 11 top posts are edited-image memes or reaction images; captions are 3–8 words; several are evergreen (Traoré, Cardio Man, Enzo triptych) and do not depend on a same-day story.
2. **One-line text-only comparisons work on X** ("Wirtz is the Garnacho of Jesse Lingard": 365K, 16K likes, text only) — the opposite of Threads where text-only is the weakest.
3. **The biggest post (1.4M) rode a same-day mega story** (City's 114/115 charges) with a "🚨 fact + best image" — a fact line tied to a fanbase punchline plus the Mourinho–Pep photo.
4. **Our own quote card template is weak** (40K; the Carrick quote card similar) — ~10–30× below the meme images. Stop it.
5. **Template fatigue is visible:** the torn-kit skinny morph went Saka 83K → Lisandro 17K in 1 day. Use any single template ≤1×/week.
6. **Follow spikes align with the top posts** [INFERRED]: 09-02 (~75 gross follows) = Enzo triptych; ~09-13/14 (~65) = Cardio Man/Traoré (09-12); 09-24/25 (~40–50) = Tottenham maths + Everton/City. So the follows come from the same few hits; ordinary days ≈ 0 net.
7. Risk: "I've seen more loyal whores" got 545K/30K likes, but X's Creator Monetization Standards list profanity/vulgarity as "restricted content" (reduced monetization) — keep vulgar posts off anything meant to earn OCR.
8. Reply desk: even the best posts got 100–230 replies; the account posts 4–16 times/day, most days no follow spike.
