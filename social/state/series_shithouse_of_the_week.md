# Series: SHITHOUSE OF THE WEEK 🏆 (v1, 2026-09-28)

**Why:** one-off virality gets views; franchises get follows (Claude-chat review 09-28). This one is literally our name, so every week tells a stranger what the account is.
**What counts as shithousery:** gamesmanship with a wink — time-wasting masterclasses, dives, wind-up celebrations, ball-boy tricks, "accidental" ball kicked away, keeper's shoelaces at 1-0 up, managers' excuses, a fanbase's meltdown, a club's PR spin. **Never:** injuries, violence, tragedy, racism/abuse, minors, private individuals.

## The weekly rhythm (Oslo)
| day | what | where | format family |
|---|---|---|---|
| through the week | every shithouse moment we see goes into `social/state/sotw/<ISO-week>.md` (clip/photo link, who, when, why it's elite) — the sweeps and live match sessions add to it | — | — |
| **Sat ~12:00** | "Shithouse of the Week — nominees" (4 options) | **X poll** + Threads/Bluesky question ("who's your shithouse of the week?") | name-answer question (the FB-pages comment magnet) |
| **Sun ~20:00** (after the day's games) | **THE AWARD**: the winner on a fixed branded card — gold "SHITHOUSE OF THE WEEK" plate, the moment's photo, 3-line citation in deadpan award language ("For services to wasting 94 seconds taking one throw-in.") | IG carousel (card first, then the moment, then runners-up, then the CTA), FB photo post (the card alone), X (card + 2–8 word line), Threads/Bluesky (card + citation) | fake-official asset |
| **Sun/Mon** | the award as a reel: card → the moment (clip that acts it out / the real still) → "Shithouse of the Week" sting, 8–12 s, white plate on IG/FB; score-card-style on YT with title "Shithouse of the Week: [Name] 🏆" | IG/FB/TikTok/YT | reel |

**Consistency is the point:** same card design, same time, same title every week (YT research: winners repeat ONE template; FB research: pages that go quiet die).

## Card template (to build: `social/sotw.mjs`)
Input: winner name, club crest (ESPN logo), photo, citation, week number. Output: 1080×1350 (IG/FB 4:5) + 1080×1920 (reel/Story). Gold/black, the week number ("Week 40"), no logo other than the award title itself.

## Measure
Follows in the 24 h after each award vs the platform's daily median; comments on the Saturday poll; by week 4 ask: is it being anticipated (people nominating unprompted)? Kill or redesign if weeks 1–3 all land below median.

## Launch
First award **Sun 4 Oct 2026** (week 40). Nominees poll Sat 3 Oct. Start logging moments from tonight's four internationals.
