## The maths, honestly
- Goal 1,000,000 by 2027-01-01. Now ≈138.6K, so +861K in 95 days = **~9,100/day**. Current pace is **~+400/day**.
- The steady-state targets below add up to ~1,500/day. That is 4× today and still 6× short of the goal.
- **So the gap has to be closed by HITS.** A post that lands in the first 1–3h of a big story gets 1M+ views, and each one brings 1–5K follows (the X 684 post: 1.36M views; the Threads Dembélé/Jude post: 228K). The whole system below exists to take **≥6 hit-shots a day** across platforms and to turn their views into follows (pinned post, bio, a follow-CTA reply under the hit).
- Re-plan on 10-12 against the scoreboard. Whichever platforms convert get the volume.

## Cross-platform engines (build/run these first)
1. **Breaking-news watcher** (build 09-28): Ornstein/Romano/ESPN every 10 min → wake the session → quote-post on X ≤15 min, Threads ≤30 min, a reel ≤3h.
2. **Matchday protocol** (every big match): ESPN watcher; an X post per moment ≤5 min; HT/FT reply rounds; FT Highlights Short ≤15 min; post-match carousel ≤25 min; FT reel.
3. **Story → 3 reels in 24h** (benchmark 09-28), each a different joke. Spread them: IG gets the best, TikTok all 3, YT the "[Club] fans…" one.
4. **Hit conversion.** Within 1h of any post passing 5× its platform median: pin it, reply under it with a follow line ("we do this every matchday 🤝"), and post a follow-up on the same story.
5. **Collabs** (IG/TikTok): IG collab posts show to both audiences. Weekly outreach to 5 meme accounts of 10–100K (the list is in outreach/). Metricool supports `collaborators`.
