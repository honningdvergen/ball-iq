## 8. X playbook additions from the extended studies (x_extended_big.md + x_extended_small.md, 09-29)
- Same-day/prev-day beats everything (46% of big-account hits tie to a match today/yesterday, 10% breaking, 7% evergreen). Small-account hits cluster on event days (28 of 84 on 09-25).
- Best timing on a mega story is NOT the first 15 min: posts 46–120 min after the scoop had median 31.6K likes vs 15.9K in the first 15 min (the same 'lawyers' joke: 14.5K at 27 min, 271K at 45 min, 137K at 82 min). Native QUOTE of the scoop account (Ornstein-type) 20–120 min after the news is a proven piggyback (6 hits from one scoop, one with zero words).
- Winning shape: reaction clip/film still or edited image mapped onto the same-day story with a 3–12 word label (film-still/celebrity substitution highest median 45K); picture-first; no hashtags, no 🚨 on humour hits.
- Ragebait = rival-bait (trophy ledgers, 'club X didn't post' tallies, claims that invite correction), NOT racially charged material (do not copy the skinhead film-still hit).
- Small accounts' winners are 65% video (reposted clips) — we do NOT copy that; our lane is images/edits/text (18%/17% of theirs, but our own top posts are images).
- 25 templates each in x_extended_big.md and x_extended_small.md: re-check every number with the fact-checker before use.
