## 2. THREE PRE-VERIFIED ABSURD-BUT-TRUE FACTS (drop the moment they're relevant)
1. **The all-time head-to-head.** Belgium 30 wins, France 29, 19 draws in 78 games (SI + Al Jazeera). Belgium haven't beaten France since **June 2015** (SI + Sports Mole), and France won all 5 since (ESPN series: 2018 1-0, 2021 3-2, Euro 2024 1-0, Sep 2024 2-0, Oct 2024 2-1).
   - So **before those 5 games it was 30-24 to Belgium, and a France win tonight makes it 30-30.** That's draft #1.
   - A draw keeps it 30-29, and a Belgium win makes it 31-29.
   - ⚠️ At FT, re-check "78 games / 30-29-19" against one more source before posting.
2. **France's Euro 2024 winner against Belgium was an own goal by Jan Vertonghen, Belgium's most-capped player** (85'). France reached that semi-final without a single open-play goal: their goals were two own goals and a penalty (Yahoo Sports, WION, ESPN report).
   - Use it if France win ugly or 1-0: "🚨 France's last knockout win over Belgium was scored by Belgium."
3. **Lukaku's price.** Chelsea paid £97.5m in Aug 2021. Napoli sold him to Fenerbahçe for **€6m** (+ up to €3m in bonuses) in Aug 2026 (ESPN, Goal/Yahoo, Türkiye Today).
   - Keep the two currencies. Don't turn it into a percentage.

**Bonus receipts (verified):**
- **Belgium's 2-0 lead in 2021.** Belgium led France 2-0 at 40' in the 2021 Nations League semi (Carrasco 37', Lukaku 40'). France won 3-2 with Theo Hernández's 90th-minute goal, and a late Lukaku goal was ruled out for offside by VAR (UEFA, Sky, SI). Use it if Belgium go 2-0 up ("Belgium have been 2-0 up on France before") or if Lukaku is flagged offside.
- **Hazard 2018:** "I prefer to lose with this Belgium than win with this France." Courtois called it "anti-football", and Belgium had 64% possession in the 1-0 semi (theScore, SI, Balls.ie).
- **Zidane's golden goal here.** Zidane scored a golden-goal penalty in THIS stadium in the Euro 2000 semi against Portugal. Barthez reminded him of it this week and Zidane said "I'd forgotten" (Walfoot + the historical record).
- **Van Bommel's discipline record.** At the 2006 World Cup v Portugal (16 yellows, 4 reds) he got Holland's first booking after 2 minutes for kicking Ronaldo's ankles, then dived for Portugal's first booking. In the 2010 final he was booked in the 22nd minute for a late tackle on Xavi (ESPN, "Mark van Bommel's recklessness won't be missed").
