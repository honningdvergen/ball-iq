## THE CRAFT per platform (from looking)

### 0. The one rule under all the others
**The clip has to ACT OUT the line: a structural pun, not just a matching mood.** The 1 goal in Brazil 1-7 Germany *is* "1 of 115" (IG-01). The Wicker Man's bees *are* Brentford (FB-02). Nemo's seagulls *are* Brighton (TT-02/03). Zootopia's country bunny arriving in the big city *is* Carlisle at the Etihad (IG-05). Our flops paired a line with a clip that only vaguely fits: hurdles for "stung by the bees" (FB-06, 34 views, against 44.5K for the literal bees), Mourinho for "City's lawyers" (OURS-01), McMahon for "therapists" (FB-05). **Reviewer test:** cover the text. Does the clip alone make a football fan think of the club, player or event? If not, the clip is wallpaper.

### IG REELS (8 rules)
1. **Frame 0 already carries the plate AND a moving clip.** 12/12 thatguysjokes reels show the full line from the very first frame. No title card, no fade-in, no logo sting (IG-01…06). *Check:* export frame 0. Is the joke readable from it alone?
2. **Plate = one sentence of 7–16 words that names a specific consequence, usually a number, a table position or a place.** "…winning 1 out of the 115 charges" (2.64M), "…move up to 19th…" (1.13M), "…Grimsby Town (a) next season" (1.05M), "Carlisle United players pulling up to the Etihad…" (462K). Our flops name no consequence: "Everton fans waking up to the City news" (OURS-02, 2.4K), "The football world is FEASTING" (OURS-03, 1.7K). *Check:* does the line contain a number, a position, a named lower-league club or a named person doing a named thing?
3. **Template A, the "white tweet plate" (thatguysjokes):** white 9:16 canvas, black system-sans text, left-aligned, 2 lines, sitting directly on top of a 16:9 clip placed in the upper-middle, with nothing else on screen (no logo, no handle, no emoji). It reads as a tweet, so it reads as a person. **Template B, the "receipt stack" (roastedballers):** black canvas, a real news tweet screenshot on top (The Touchline's list of champions), a bold quote line under it, then a clip with club crests stuck on the characters. B is what lets a cold viewer get the joke (IG-07 3.7M, IG-08 1.9M). *Check:* is the draft visibly A or B? Anything else (blurred-background fill, text on the clip, two text blocks) has failed (OURS-04/05).
4. **6–18 s, loopable.** The IG winners run 6–18 s (median ≈11 s). Our 36-second statues reel (OURS-01) is 3× too long. *Check:* ≤15 s unless the audio is a story that needs the time.
5. **When the clip has iconic audio, keep it: the audio is the second punchline.** The Oscar commentary "…Brazil one, Germany seven" lands the 1-of-115 joke (IG-01). Wolf of Wall Street: "Can you say that again? Just the way you say" (IG-06). 2 Broke Girls: "You're in my house now" as Brighton turn it round (IG-12). Otherwise use a trending song that matches the emotion (ELO "I'm Alive" for the apes; Drake "God's Plan" for "PL lawyers" giving United money; Lil Wayne "Let It All Work Out" for Arsenal's sadness). *Check:* write the sound down in the draft. Is it (a) the clip's own line that pays off the plate, or (b) a named trending song whose mood matches?
6. **Crest stickers + a live scoreboard make the clip a match report.** fc.maverick's scoreboard ticks from 2-0 (11') to 2-3 FT while the sitcom fight plays (IG-12, 1.5M at 176× median). roastedballers' Vinícius rating card "6.4" sits in the corner of the treadmill clip (IG-10, 555K). *Check:* when the joke is about a result, is the score on screen?
7. **No line = no reel.** Same account, same match, same template, same day: "'I'll do it 10x' highlights vs Atlético" + Vini rating = 555K. Crests only, no line (Barça fans dancing) = **6.1K** (IG-10 vs IG-11). *Check:* never publish a crest-only or music-only reel.
8. **Volume on one story, but a new angle each time, and first.** thatguysjokes posted 7 City reels in ~24 h, each a different consequence (lawyer, Spurs 19th, Carlisle, Grimsby, Ornstein, courtroom, Tranmere). roastedballers' 4th City reel needed two inference steps (VVD takes Aké's 21/22 medal) and died at 20K, 3–5% of their median (IG-09). Our lawyer reel came after the same joke was already out (OURS-01, 6.1K). *Check:* has this exact premise already gone out today on thatguysjokes, roastedballers or rivalsbanter? If yes, pick a different consequence.

### TIKTOK (7 rules)
1. **Text in TikTok's native caption style:** white bold sans with a black stroke, or a white box, centred in the upper third, never in a white bar. 7/8 outliers use it (TT-01, 02, 04–08). Our white-bar IG template reads as a repost there. *Check:* make the TikTok copy with in-app text or an identical style.
2. **6–8 s.** hellosky 6.6 s, croatball 7.8 s, iduate 7.7 s, lifetokhq 7.4 s, kamelius 6.5 s, all 1.3–2.1M. Only the apes (12.5 s) and the Ornstein-WWE build-up (17.7 s) run longer, and both are driven by music. *Check:* ≤8 s unless the song's drop needs more.
3. **The sound is part of the joke, not a bed.** Nemo's "Mine! Mine!" / the "pecked by the seagulls" chant (TT-02/03), ELO "I'm Alive" (TT-01), the entrance music plus a crowd pop for Ornstein (TT-04), J. Cole on the kit rail (TT-05). *Check:* with the sound on, does the clip get funnier? A silent-copy upload from us must name the exact sound for Alex to add.
4. **A landscape clip letterboxed in black is fine.** TT-01 and TT-02 are plain 16:9 inside black bars, and TT-02 is the whole frame at 16:9. Don't waste time reframing; spend it on the pun.
5. **Follower count is irrelevant, story timing is everything.** homageboy (441 followers) 1.5M, kamelius (475) 1.3M, iduate (short channel) 2.1M, all within ~24 h of the story they ride.
6. **Half of TikTok's outliers are face-to-camera skits** (POV kit rail, Pep-in-the-car, "calm down", phone call; TT-05…08). We have no face. Our lane is the film-clip pun (TT-01…04), which is the other half.
7. **Caption = 0–5 words + 4–6 hashtags naming the club and the story** (#115 #manchestercity #premierleague #relegation #spurs). No sentence.
⚠️ Unresolved: TT-01/02 re-use film clips and still reached 1.3–1.5M. Our rule "re-uploads = ineligible" is about Creator Rewards eligibility and the "unoriginal" label, not virality. Keep sending Alex a film-pun draft with our own plate text and native caption; don't re-upload match footage.

### YT SHORTS (6 rules)
1. **Title = the plate line, in fan-speak, plus 💀/😂 and 3–5 hashtags.** "Man City Fans Seeing the 115 Charges News 💀😂" (192K, 456 subs, 102×), "Every football fans Reaction to Manchester city 115 Charges" (257K), "Manchester City fixtures next season🤣" (181K). *Check:* does the title start with a fanbase or a club and name the story keyword (115 / charges / Arsenal 3-0)?
2. **First frame = bold white text on black at the top + a famous TV/film face below** (The Office's Brent, Michael Sheen, the Simpsons with PL and City crests; YT-01…03). The first frame is the thumbnail.
3. **Ride the flood within ~1–2 h.** The City Shorts that broke out went up 17:08–18:03 Oslo, 1–2 h after the story. Two days later only novelty angles still broke out (YT-05 FC-card animation 375K on 09-27).
4. **The same joke copied later does ~1% as well.** Mowler posted the identical "lawyer winning 1 out of the 115" clip + plate on YT at 18:03 → **31K**. thatguysjokes' IG original got 2.64M. Ours on YT got 31 views (YT-04). Be first or be different.
5. **Loop to 100%+ watched.** Our only YT hits ("Brighton vs Arsenal highlights" 1,397 at 129% watched, "Spurs fans after going three down" 1,180 at 132%) went out the night of the match and looped. Everything later got 17–109 views.
6. **Tiny channels win.** 344–456-sub channels got 43K–192K on the story. Our 30 subs is not the excuse.

### FB REELS (6 rules, from OUR Page: 4 hits of 40–80K vs a floor of 25–60 views)
1. **FB is binary:** a reel either escapes to non-followers (40–80K) or dies at ~30. The 4 escapes share one trait: the clip is funny **with no football knowledge** (fat blokes falling over, "NOT THE BEES!", a rude donation name read out loud, a maths reveal). FB's audience is broader and older than IG's.
2. **Plate = 3–10 words, bold, above the clip.** "Tottenham vs Aston Villa" (78.6K) is the whole plate. The clip does the talking.
3. **Literal meme vessels beat metaphors:** "every chelsea fan who called brentford away a free three points" + Wicker Man bees = **44.5K**. "'Title contenders' Chelsea just got stung by the bees" + a hurdler falling = **34**. Same story, same week (FB-02 vs FB-06).
4. **Audio punchline on FB too:** Nicolas Cage's scream, Goldbridge reading "Icewallowcom" aloud (FB-04, 42K).
5. **Speed + our own receipt card:** the Everton-6-points / City-114 maths card as a two-beat text reveal ("That's 684 points.") went out ~30 min after the story → **42.3K, 499 likes** (FB-03). A static card fails when it's late or doesn't move. This one was first and revealed in 2 beats.
6. **A near-static clip dies:** the disappointed-cricket-fan clip barely moves over 4.6 s → 27 views (FB-07). Whatever the platform, the clip must MOVE in second 1.

## INSTANT FAILS (each backed by one of our flops or an in-account flop)
- **Clip is a mood, not a pun** → FB-06 hurdles for "bees" (34 views), OURS-01 Mourinho montage for "lawyers" (6.1K), FB-05 McMahon for "therapists" (26).
- **The clip's own person steals the joke** (a famous football face that isn't the subject) → OURS-01: viewers see Mourinho and ask "why Mourinho?".
- **Two text blocks / plate changes mid-reel / stat restated** → FB-05 "therapists…" then "five games. no wins. bottom of the league" (26 views). OURS-05 "NORWAY 1 2 PORTUGAL" + "Norway's finishing vs Portugal" (1.5K, 0 shares).
- **Blurred-background fill with the clip shrunk to ~35% of the frame** → OURS-04 Pep/Manuel (1.1K), OURS-05 (1.5K), which looks low-effort next to the white-plate and receipt-stack templates.
- **>20 s for a one-beat joke** → OURS-01 36 s.
- **Posted after the same premise is out** → OURS-01 statues (lawyer joke), OURS-03 feasting (3rd lawyer-angle reel from us that day), YT Mowler copy (31K vs 2.64M).
- **Story is 2+ days old** → OURS-04 Pep reel 09-27 on a 09-25 story (1.1K); City carousels day 2–4 (2–5K).
- **Descriptive plate, no consequence** → OURS-02 "Everton fans waking up to the City news" (2.4K IG, 54 FB).
- **No line at all** → IG-11 roastedballers crest-only (6.1K vs 555K).
- **Two-step inference** → IG-09 VVD/Aké medal (20K on a ~450K-median account).
- **Near-static clip** → FB-07 (27 views).
- **Mixed-topic carousel** → CAR-04 (2.3K, 0 shares).
