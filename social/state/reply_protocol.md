# Reply protocol — fast replies under viral posts WITHOUT looking inauthentic (Alex 09-29: "there has to be a method to the chaos")
Goal: reach + follows from replies, zero spam flags. Owner: Editor. PM audits (floor_<date>.md).
## Rules
1. **Only a reply that is funny/sharp on its own** (would be a good standalone tweet). Sarcasm/ragebait allowed (risk-appetite rule); never discrimination, tragedy, serious injury, minors, defamation.
2. **Unique every time**: never the same or near-same text twice in 7 days (check replies.md), no templates, no "follow us", no links, no hashtags, no @-tag chains, no emoji spam.
3. **Human pacing**: X ≤ 4 replies/hour and ≤ 30/day, random 4–15 min gaps, spread over waking hours in UK/EU/US time (not a burst); Threads ≤ 15/day; Bluesky ≤ 5/day; IG comments ≤ 8/day on big posts only (IG is strictest).
4. **Targets**: posts of big accounts (news/banter/fan) that are <30 min old and already moving (>200 likes in 20 min); max 2 replies per account per day; skip accounts that blocked/muted us, skip sensitive stories.
5. **Never reply to our own replies with a link/CTA; never mass-reply the same post**; one reply per thread.
6. **Tripwires**: any "unusual activity"/rate-limit/CAPTCHA/verification prompt, or a sudden drop in our impressions → STOP all replies on that platform for 24 h and tell the PM.
7. **Log** every reply in social/state/replies.md (date · platform · account/thread id · text) so repeats are checkable.
8. Posted via Chrome by the Editor (the account is Alex's brand identity: never on a personal profile); Bluesky replies via social/bsky.mjs.
