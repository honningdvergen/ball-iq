## TL;DR

1. **Hater Central is one creator on his second account.**
   - The main account, @TheHateCentral (about 650K followers, joined Apr 2022, started as an NBA troll account), was **suspended on 2026-07-15**, during the World Cup.
   - He came back on @TheHateCentral2 (a backup created 2022-07-02) on **22 Aug**. It had **161.8K followers by 24 Sep**. That is about **4.9K a day** [est], and roughly a quarter of the old count, which is what his pinned "1/4 of way there" means.
2. **No public source gives the suspension reason.** He only said "after other account got suspended". The most likely reason is **repeat copyright strikes** [inference]:
   - the new account **already has two videos "disabled in response to a report by the copyright owner"** (19 and 20 Sep) [primary];
   - it posts clips carrying TF1, beIN and Eurosport bugs [primary];
   - the old account's signature was "fail comps", compilations of match footage [1 src];
   - X's policy is to suspend repeat infringers [primary policy text via search].
3. **His engine has three parts:**
   - **Volume on matchdays.** 15–16 posts cleared 10K likes on each of 19 and 20 Sep.
   - **Three repeatable formats:** stat-sheet roasts, quote-post receipts, and meme-video reactions.
   - **A community event, #BallonDont.** It uses a nominee drip, sub-awards, voting windows and a ceremony date. It produced his biggest reply counts (2,533 on one text post) and his biggest image reach (1.78M views on the nominee graphic).
4. **What we can copy safely:** the event mechanics, stat-sheet roasts, receipts quote-posts, quote-posts of news, and matchday volume. **What we can't:** his video re-uploads. They are the suspension risk, and they earn **zero** Original Content Rewards (OCR).
5. **Our gap is volume, not wit.** Over the last 8 days search shows **11 originals from us (about 1.4 a day)** against his roughly 11 a day. Our same-format post ("Bukayo Saka vs Brighton:", 3.1K likes / 82K views) arrived after his Saka post had already done 59.5K / 2.56M [primary].
6. **Signature event for us: THE GOLDEN TOWEL.** A weekly award for football's best shithouse (the dark arts, not hate), crowned by native poll. The first monthly Towel is handed out on **Ballon d'Or night (26 Oct)**, so we ride the biggest football trend of the autumn.

**Frequency.** 366 posts in about 33 days is roughly **11 a day, replies included** [est]. On matchdays (19 and 20 Sep) he posted **15–16 posts that each cleared 10K likes**, clustered from kickoff to about 4h after full time. International-break days drop to 2–3 hits.

**Trend-riding**
- He quotes the **biggest news post of the day**. Fabrizio's Mbappé–Africa post went up at 10:56 UTC with 7.47M views and 5.5K replies. His quote followed at 13:27, **2.5h later**, and still did 1.2M views [primary]. For a news hook, landing on the biggest post matters more than being first.
- Receipts are **latency-free**: he quotes old posts (2023–24) the moment a match makes them funny.
- He @-tags players, clubs, @FIFA, @UEFA and @ballondor in roasts. That draws those fanbases into the replies.

**#BallonDont: how the event works** [primary]

1. **A nominee drip**, one post per nominee, each tagging the player's club and national team:
   - Ronaldo: **42.6K likes / 818 replies**
   - Foden, billed as the "final nominee": 10.3K / 1.14M views
   - Then a fake-out, "The *actual* final nominee", 8.0K / 599K
2. **The full nominee graphic**, 30 players: **30.8K / 1.78M views / 661 replies**.
3. **Crowd-sourced sub-awards.** "GK of the Year nominees 30 mins. Drop nominations ASAP" (332 replies) became the "Yashit" goalkeeper award (25.3K).
4. **The rules as a graphic:** pick your top 5 in order, scored 8/5/3/2/1.
5. **Voting windows.** A 24h text post, "Comment your top 5… ✨RT", drew **2,533 replies / 9.9K likes / 178K views**. There are also "Over 1k votes in first 30 minutes" updates and a countdown ("VOTING OPENS IN 2 HOURS").
6. **Extra voting platforms** (IG and TikTok, "vote once on all platforms for up to 3 votes") move the audience across his accounts.
7. **A ceremony date** (30 Oct, London) gives a reason to follow.

**Why it works [inference]**
- A reply is **the vote itself**, and a reply is weighted 5 in X's published ranking, against 0.5 for a like.
- Every nominee has a fanbase that turns up to defend them.
- The drip turns one idea into about 30 posts.
- The scheduled windows create appointments.

**Tone.** "Hate" here means **performance-roasting**: stat sheets, nicknames ("Bricklayer G", "Saddam Huijsen", "Lemon Yamal") and irony ("What a tackle by Rice 👏 POTM soon", 43.3K). It is not abuse. The target is always a *performance*, never a background or private life.

## 3. Risks: what got him suspended, and what we can copy

**X copyright policy** (help page, via search, 2026-09-24):
- Multiple complaints "or other evidence suggests a pattern of repeat infringement" can mean suspension under the Repeat Infringer Policy.
- Re-posting media that was already removed "may result in permanent account suspension".

The new account already carries **two copyright-disabled videos** ("This buildup from United", 20 Sep, 16.1K; "Bricklayer G vs Brighton", 19 Sep, 16.8K) [primary]. The **FIFA World Cup** was the most heavily enforced footage of the year: the DOJ seized about 400 streaming domains in June [1 src]. The suspension date sits inside it.

**Verdict:** the reason is **unconfirmed** (0 of 2 sources). "Repeat copyright strikes on video" is the best-supported hypothesis [inference]. **Do not state it as fact** anywhere.

**How OCR treats his playbook** (see `x_ocr_and_replies_2026_09.md`):
- Re-uploads, clips from other platforms, and captioned meme videos ("minimally modified") are **ineligible**.
- The duplicate model gives those impressions to the original uploader.
- His monetisation is "DM for Promo", not OCR [inference from bio].

| His tactic | For us | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Community award with nominee drip, windows, ceremony | **COPY (own twist)** | The graphics are ours, so they count for OCR, and the replies are the votes |
| Stat-sheet roast (text plus photo) | **COPY** | Our words; numbers must be [VERIFY]d (FotMob/Sofascore) |
| Receipts: quote-posting old public takes | **COPY** | A native quote-post is commentary. Only quote public figures and pundits, never small private accounts |
| Quote-posting Fabrizio, clubs and broadcasters' *own* clip posts | **COPY** | The clip stays on the rights-holder's post |
| Live text-only lines during matches | **COPY** | Zero rights risk |
| Tagging clubs and national teams on award posts | **COPY, clubs only** | Tagging *players* in roasts invites reports (−234) and blocks (−31.2) |
| Uploading broadcast clips (TF1, beIN, Eurosport bugs) or "fail comps" | **NEVER** | This is the suspension path |
| Captioned meme videos of other people's footage | **NEVER** | Copied or minimally modified, so 0 OCR |
| "✨RT" / "comment to vote" in every window | **ADAPT** | Engagement solicitation 3+ times means OCR removal. We use **native polls**, plus at most **one** reply-nomination call a week, and never ask for RT, like or follow [inference from the 07-16 rule] |
| PL-logo parody ("Team of the Weakened") | **AVOID** | Uses a trademark; our own frame is safer |

## 6. The 30-day plan (25 Sep → 26 Oct)

### Signature event: THE GOLDEN TOWEL 🏆

**What it is.** The award for football's best **shithouse**: the time-waster, the wind-up merchant, the sly foul, the admin jab. The frame is **affectionate dark-arts appreciation**, not hate. That makes fanbases *argue* ("that's not shithousery, that's cheating") instead of mute. It is also our name, so every post advertises the handle. It isn't a copy of the Ballon d'Ont: we reward the dark arts, where Hater Central punishes bad play.

**Weekly cycle (from the first PL weekend back after the break) [VERIFY fixture dates]**

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| **Sun night / Mon** | **Nominee drip**: 4 posts, one per nominee, each our own card (player photo + gold "NOMINATED" frame + a one-line charge sheet), tagging the **club** only |
| **Tue 12:30** | **Native poll**, 4 options, 24h: "Golden Towel, Week N" |
| **Wed 17:30** | **Winner card** (own trophy graphic) + a 3-word verdict |
| **Once a week** | "DM us the shithousery we missed". Fan submissions are **leads only**: never re-upload their clip; quote the rights-holder's post or use a still |

**Side award: Admin of the Week.** Club social accounts jabbing each other, like Sunderland's corner joke that did 64.7K for us. Quote-post the original. Admins tend to reply when they're tagged, which is the collaboration lever.

**Finale: the first Golden Towel of the Month**
- The 4 weekly winners go head to head in a bracket poll.
- The winner is crowned **Mon 26 Oct, Ballon d'Or night** (London [VERIFY date and time]). The caption is: "Paris gives out a golden ball. We give out a golden towel."
- This lands on the night football's biggest trend peaks, 4 days before Hater Central's ceremony.

**Guardrails**
- **No "RT / like / follow to vote"**, and at most one reply-CTA a week (OCR's 3-strike rule).
- **At most one tag (#GoldenTowel)**, and only on event posts.
- No tragedy, injury or private life. Nominate *acts*, never people's character.
- **Every graphic is ours**, so all of it counts for OCR.
