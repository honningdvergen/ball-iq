Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version: Play as a guest and your progress lives on your device; the only thing we receive is your daily puzzle result, counted anonymously so everyone can see how the day went. Sign in and we store the minimum needed to sync your account across devices: email, username, scores, and game history. We may send a few reminder emails and notifications about Ball IQ, each easy to switch off. The app shows no ads, and counts how often features are used with no identifier of any kind attached. Our website (balliq.app) uses privacy-friendly cookieless analytics, records a few first-party product events, and uses Microsoft Clarity session analytics and PostHog product analytics — which, in Europe, we load only if you say yes. We are not currently showing ads anywhere, and the app itself stays ad-free and cookie-free. We never sell your data.

1. Two ways to play

Guest mode — open Ball IQ without signing in. Your scores, streaks, settings and game history are stored only in your device's local storage. The one thing sent to our servers is your result when you finish a daily puzzle, so the “how everyone did” panel works: from the app it carries no identifier at all, and on the website it carries the random visitor identifier described in §4. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, your progress is gone. If you join a game or a league from an invite link without signing in, we create a temporary guest account with a username so the game works; it is deleted automatically after 30 days without use.

Signed-in mode — sign in with email, Apple or Google to back up your progress, play online multiplayer and join leagues. We store the minimum needed to deliver those features (detail in §2 below). You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account. That deletes your account, profile, scores, game history, friends, league memberships, notification settings and email history, and removes your link to anything that stays in our database: your past daily puzzle results remain only as anonymous counts, and any question you reported stays as an anonymous report about that question.

2. What we store when you sign in

When you have an account, the following lives on our servers (hosted by Supabase, see §5):

  • Email address and sign-in method — used for sign-in (password, Apple or Google), account emails such as confirming your address, and a small number of reminder emails about Ball IQ: for example a nudge on your second day, or a note if you haven't played for a while. These are about Ball IQ only — no newsletters, no offers from anyone else — and every one has a one-tap link to unsubscribe from all Ball IQ emails. We keep a log of which reminder emails we sent you so you never get the same one twice.
  • Username, avatar and scores — public within Ball IQ: other players can see them in friend search, profiles, leaderboards and leagues.
  • Game results — Daily 7 scores, Footle history, daily puzzle results, total games played, total XP, best streaks, and the questions you got wrong (for the review screen).
  • Friendships, leagues and multiplayer — friend requests sent + accepted, the leagues you join and your points in them, and the rooms and answers of online games you play.
  • Notifications — if you allow notifications, the push address for your device or browser, its platform, your time-zone offset and your preferred reminder hour, so reminders arrive at a sensible time. Apple and Google deliver notifications to phones, and your browser's own push service delivers them on the website. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device settings.
  • Question reports — if you report a question as wrong, the question, the answer you picked and your account ID, so we can fix it.
  • Onboarded flag — a single timestamp so we don't replay onboarding on every device.
  • Where you first found us — if you create your account on the website, we keep the campaign tag and referring site of your first visit (for example utm_source=instagram or google.com) and the page you landed on, so we can tell which channels bring players. Only the site's name, never the full address you came from. It is not visible to anyone, including you, and accounts created in the app don't have it.

That's the full list. We do not collect your real name, location, device fingerprint, or any contact list. We never sell or share this data with third parties for marketing.

3. Error monitoring

We use Sentry to catch crashes and bugs. When something goes wrong in the app, Sentry receives a stack trace, the browser version, and a short trail of recent actions ("breadcrumbs") — no question text, no personal data. We strip your email and auth tokens from every event before it leaves your device. We use this only to fix bugs, never for tracking.

4. Analytics & ads

The Ball IQ app uses no third-party analytics tools, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs. It shows no ads and works with no ad networks. This is enforced in code: the ad and analytics scripts are blocked from ever loading inside the app.

The app does count, anonymously, how often its features are used — for example “a daily puzzle was finished” — so we can tell which parts are worth improving. These counts carry no identifier of any kind: no device id, no advertising id, and not your account either. Nothing links one count to another, so they cannot be assembled into a picture of you, and we have no way to look up what any individual person did. This is enforced on our server rather than only in the app, so it holds even for an older version still installed on a device.

Our website (balliq.app) uses Vercel Web Analytics — a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics service — to understand aggregate traffic, such as how many people visit a page. It sets no cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not identify you personally.

Our website also records a small number of first-party product events of our own — for example “a quiz was started” or “the app link was tapped” — so we can tell which parts of the site are working. Each event stores only its name, the surface it happened on, the campaign tag of the link that brought you there if it had one (for example utm_source=instagram), and a random identifier we generate on your device (stored in your browser as biq_vid) so a single visit hangs together. That identifier is a random value, not derived from anything about you or your device: it is not a fingerprint and it is not shared with anyone. If you are signed in on the website, these events are also linked to your account ID (never your email); deleting your account removes that link. Clearing this site’s data removes it. No question text and no personal data is recorded. This runs on the website only.

Our website (balliq.app) also uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site — it captures anonymised interaction signals (clicks, scrolls, mouse movement) and session replays with typed text masked, which we use to find and fix confusing parts of the site. Clarity may set cookies and similar identifiers. This runs on the website only — never in the app. If you are visiting from Europe, Clarity does not run at all unless you allow it: we ask you once, on your first visit, and nothing loads until you choose. Choosing “Decline” means Clarity is never loaded, and the rest of the site works exactly the same. You can change your mind by clearing this site’s data in your browser, which makes us ask again. See Microsoft's privacy statement at privacy.microsoft.com.

Our website (balliq.app) also uses PostHog product analytics (EU cloud, Frankfurt) to measure which games people play, where they stop, and whether they come back, and to compare versions of a page. It records page views, clicks and the same product events described above, with IP addresses anonymised; it does not record session replays. If you are signed in, events are linked to your account ID (never your email or username). PostHog stores an identifier in your browser. It follows exactly the same rules as Clarity: website only, never in the app, and in Europe only if you choose “Allow”. See PostHog's privacy policy at posthog.com/privacy.

We do not currently display ads anywhere — not on the website and not in the app. If that changes we will update this page first. The paragraph below describes how Google AdSense would work on the website only if we enable it. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may set and read cookies or similar identifiers in your browser, and — where you agree — use them to show more relevant ads. This applies to the website only; the app remains ad-free and sets no advertising cookies. We are not currently serving ads, so no advertising cookies are set. The consent prompt European visitors see covers Microsoft Clarity and PostHog only, described above — it is not an advertising prompt. Before we enable ads for visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we will ask for your consent choices first, and you will be able to change them at any time. You can review how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

5. Third-party services we use

  • Supabase (database, auth, storage): When you sign in, your email, username, profile data, game results and avatar image are stored on Supabase servers (EU region). Supabase is our data processor.
  • Sentry (error monitoring): Crash reports are sent to Sentry with email and tokens scrubbed before transmission.
  • Vercel (hosting + website analytics): Serves Ball IQ over HTTPS and may log standard request data (IP, timestamp, URL) for operational purposes. On our website (balliq.app) we also use Vercel Web Analytics for aggregate, cookieless usage metrics — no cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal identification.
  • Google AdSense (not currently enabled): We do not serve ads today. If the website later displays ads through Google AdSense, Google and its ad partners may set cookies or similar identifiers to serve, measure, and (with consent) personalise ads. This runs on the website only — never in the app. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity (website session analytics): Captures anonymised interaction data on balliq.app (clicks, scrolls, session replays with typed text masked) to help us improve the site. May set cookies. Website only — never in the app, and in Europe only with your consent. See privacy.microsoft.com.
  • PostHog (website product analytics): Measures page views, clicks and product events on balliq.app (EU cloud, IPs anonymised, no session replays) to help us improve the site and test changes. Stores an identifier in your browser. Website only — never in the app, and in Europe only with your consent. See posthog.com/privacy.
  • Resend (email delivery): Sends our reminder emails. It receives your email address and the message. Resend is our data processor.
  • Apple and Google (sign-in and notifications): If you sign in with Apple or Google, they confirm your identity to us. If you allow notifications, Apple's and Google's push services deliver them to your device.
  • Google Fonts: We load the Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts from fonts.googleapis.com. Google may log your IP address when fonts are fetched.
  • Cropper.js (CDN): When you upload a profile picture, we load an image-cropping library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. The CDN provider may log your IP address.

6. How long we keep data, and where

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Website product events are deleted automatically after 180 days, and unused guest accounts after 30 days. Our database (Supabase), error monitoring (Sentry) and product analytics (PostHog) are hosted in the EU. Some services we use are US companies (Vercel, Microsoft, Resend); where data leaves the EEA, it is covered by the safeguards those companies provide, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

7. Children's Privacy

Ball IQ is not directed at children under 13. Guest mode requires no account or personal data. To sign in you must provide an email address — please don't sign in if you are under 13. The app's content is suitable for all ages.
