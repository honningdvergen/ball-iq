## Design specificity verdict

**Authored on one URL, category-interchangeable everywhere else — and the authored page has the wrong chrome around it.**

- `/` is a real idea (paper report on a dark desk, ledger that fills itself in, a 176px verdict) executed at ~70%. Nobody else in football-quiz land looks like this.
- Every generated page (`/football-quiz/`, `/quiz/*`, `/lists/*`) is the 2023 indie-SaaS template: Inter on #0A0A0A, eleven distinct border radii on the Arsenal page alone (9–24px + pill), JetBrains Mono eyebrows, lime pill CTA, an orange-gradient "Prove it" banner with a flame. Swap the copy for a crypto quiz and nothing changes. **100% of Google traffic lands here, 0% on the authored page.**
- The app shell (`/play`, `/footle`) is a third world: sidebar, glowing lime ENTER bar, emoji in the h1.
- Detector, deterministic (45 CLI findings, all warnings): Inter at 86–100% of text nodes on every page; `dark-glow` on every green CTA; **222 undersized-text (10px) hits on `/quiz/arsenal/`**, 95 on `/football-quiz/`; 18 tight-leading hits on `/lists/`; low-contrast white-on-club-red 4.3–4.5:1. Its in-page pass independently found the 1.1:1 lede.

**What "doesn't look right" about the report sheet (Alex's instinct, confirmed at 2×):** it looks *disabled*. Newsprint #E7E9E4 is a grey, and on it sit grey option boxes with grey borders, grey letter chips, grey zebra stripes, grey "not assessed" ×5 — every surface within ~10 L* of the next, so the whole object reads as a greyed-out form. The black ASSESSMENT bar + 1px box + boxed chips read as a tax form, not a scout's report; there is no football object on it at all (zero `<img>` on the page). The −0.45° rotation produces jaggy edges and stripes that run off-horizontal against a perfectly horizontal browser, and the backing sheet shows as a thin grey sliver, so the "stack" reads as a misalignment. The grain reads as pixel noise on the letterhead. Type is small on a big sheet (20px question on a 1069px sheet; ~40% empty margin). Ledger labels sit ~6px below their values. And the lime pill + lime "IQ" above it belong to another product.

## Overall impression

Fast, clean, zero console errors, zero failed requests, no horizontal overflow, honest touch targets. Someone who cares built this. But it is three products stitched at the URL boundary, the one distinctive page ships with its most important sentence and its climax button invisible (both fixed and live today), and the pages that receive the traffic are the least designed. The single biggest opportunity: **pick ONE world and make the question sheet — the thing people actually use — the shared component on every page.**

## What's working

1. **The ledger + verdict mechanic** — `not assessed → 0 of 1 / 1 of 1`, ending in a 176px coloured number with a tier name. A document that fills itself in is a better end state than a score screen.
2. **The WHY panel** — every answer explains itself, typographically the best-set block on the site.
3. **The club-quiz mobile fold** — badge, ARSENAL QUIZ, today's-set strip, the full question and four options inside 844px. The correct answer to "playable beats readable"; the rest of the site should copy it.
4. **Craft floor** — WebKit FCP ~317ms, 0 errors, 0 failed requests, 44–60px targets, static `<title>`/`<h1>` in raw HTML.

## Questions to consider
