# Assessment A — Design Review of balliq.app (live) — 2026-09-05

Observations appended per page as inspected. Synthesis at the end.

## Page 1 — https://balliq.app/ (phone 375×812; one screenshot captured before the pane went hidden, rest via DOM/computed-style probes)

**What's there.** Sticky 57px header: crest (26×26 tap target), search "Find your club or league", hamburger. Date line "Saturday, 5 September 2026 / Footle No. 125 · Transfer Trail No. 34 · Mystery Player No. 34". Then 5 stacked sections: Today (4 puzzle cards, each with a green pill "Play", "0 of 4 played · new ones in 13h 11m"), Your club (10 league chips + 16 club tiles + "Every club on file / Search for one"), Every game (14 rows), Lists and records (8 rows), How it works (4 FAQs). Footer: 71 links in 6 columns (Games / Premier League / La Liga / Serie A / Bundesliga / Lists). Page is 5,470px tall = **6.7 phone screens**. Only fonts: Inter (+ Arial fallback somewhere).

**Good.** The first screen is genuinely authored: date line + puzzle numbers is a real "newspaper masthead" move and the Footle KROOS/RAMOS tile motif is the only visual that is unmistakably football-and-this-product. "0 of 4 played · new ones in 13h" is a proper state indicator. One accent colour (green) spent only on Play — matches DESIGN.md's stated rule. Copy is human ("nothing about today's is given away").

**Wrong / weak.**
- **Two destinations for the same thing.** The 16 club tiles in "Your club" link to `/play?club=arsenal` (the app), while the 53 links elsewhere (footer, chips) go to `/quiz/arsenal/` (the static website page). Same club, two different products depending on which pixel you tapped. This is the fragmentation the owner feels, in one screen. Goal 1 + 2.
- **Every "Play" leaves the website.** All 4 Today cards and all 14 "Every game" rows point at `/play?game=…`. The homepage is a menu for a different app; the website has no game of its own here. Goal 1.
- **Hamburger = anchors + "Sign in".** Menu contents: Today | Games | Clubs | Quizzes | Lists | Sign in. Four of six are in-page anchors (`/#today`); "Sign in" (359×45, full-width, the most prominent item) goes to `/play` — it does not sign anyone in, it opens the app. Mislabelled control. Goal 2, 4.
- **Football is nearly absent below the fold.** Club tiles are text-only ("Arsenal / Premier League") — no crest, no colour, no badge. 93 clubs and the section looks like a settings list. "Your club" implies personalisation but nothing is personalised (no remembered club, no geo hint). Goal 3.
- **Decision overload.** Every game = 14 rows in one flat list, no grouping between daily/timed/multiplayer beyond a tiny DAILY tag; Footle/Daily 7/Trail/Mystery appear *twice* on the same page (Today and Every game). Cognitive-load: ≤4 choices at a decision point fails; chunking fails.
- **Dead CTAs at the bottom.** "Every club on file" and "Search for one" are `<button>`s with no visible affordance difference; on the 6.7-screen page the search is 5 screens above.
- **Stale seams.** DOM contains a hidden legacy landing (`.landing-features` "What's inside" with 🧠 📅 🤝 emoji, "Take Ball IQ with you" App Store/Google Play block) and a hidden "Lionel Messi / Cristiano Ronaldo / Pelé / Neymar / Skip / Start playing" taster — old product surfaces shipped but display:none. Two generations of homepage coexist in the HTML.
- **Lists rows** show "checked 20 July · with a quiz" — an internal QA date surfaced to users; the word "checked" means nothing to Jordan. "Lists and records" also uses tiny 20px-tall link targets ("All 50 lists" 68×20).
- **h1 is visually hidden** (sr-only) — the page has no visible headline at all; the date is doing the job of the H1. Bold, but Jordan landing cold never reads the words "football quiz".
- **App funnel on the homepage: invisible.** "Get the app→/get" exists only inside the hidden nav item; the App Store / Google Play block is in the display:none legacy section. Zero visible native-app mention on the front door. Goal 4 fails by absence, not pushiness.
- Tap targets: league chips 36px tall (below 44), lists links 20px tall, crest 26px.

### Page 1 — desktop 1440×900 (screenshot captured)
- Layout: 1120px column centred, 160px gutters. Header 61px: wordmark + Today/Games/Clubs/Quizzes/Lists + search (centred, ~230px) + "Sign in". Calm, legible, competent. Page 2,456px tall.
- **Above the fold** (900px): date line, Today (4 cards in an *uneven* 2-col grid: 589px + 471px — the right column is narrower for no reason, so Daily 7's description wraps to 2 lines and the card is taller than its neighbour), archive link, "Your club" title + 10 chips + the first row of tiles. 105 visible text nodes above the fold — dense but scannable.
- Club tiles at desktop get a **club-colour dot** (red Arsenal, blue Chelsea, white Real Madrid) — this is the *only* football colour on the whole page, and it's an 8px dot. The tile is otherwise a grey card with two lines of text. The site owns 93 clubs and shows them as a directory listing.
- "Every game" = 3-col grid of 93px-tall rows, 14 of them, five of which duplicate the Today block directly above. Identical visual weight for "Footle" (the flagship) and "Chaos".
- Footer: 8 equal 115px columns of link lists (Games, PL, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Lists, Discover, Ball IQ) — sitemap-as-footer. Closing line "Also on iOS and Android · An independent football quiz, made by one person." is the *only* visible native-app mention on the front door, and it's a footer caption with no link on "iOS and Android" as far as the text shows.
- The whole page renders as one typeface at three weights, one grey scale, one green. Design-system-consistent, yes; but at 1440 it reads as a dark developer dashboard (Vercel/Linear register), not a football site. Nothing here would be wrong on a SaaS status page.

## Page 2 — https://balliq.app/quiz/arsenal/ (phone 375: DOM probes, pane hidden for screenshots; desktop 1440: screenshot captured). Played all 10 questions to the finish screen (2/10 deliberately).

**Structure (phone).** 6,757px = **8.3 screens**. Header is *different from the homepage header at the same width*: crest + hamburger only, no search field (homepage phone header has search). Breadcrumb Home › Quizzes › Arsenal. Red "ARS" crest-substitute badge (47px, rgb(229,1,7)) + "CLUB QUIZ" eyebrow + H1 "Arsenal Quiz" 34px + 3-line SEO blurb + **App Store / Google Play badges (145×52, 159×52) at y≈1000 on phone / y=439 on desktop** + stat strip "Free always · 100% explained · Daily fresh set" + "Free to play · no sign-up" caption. Then the quiz card (335px wide; options 293px, 49–96px tall, 14.5px text). Then "Think you know Arsenal? Prove it." block with a green "Play free in your browser →" (→ `/play?club=arsenal`) + **a second pair of store badges**. Then "More quizzes to try" (24 three-letter-code tiles + "Show all 135 quizzes"), printable-list link, Bahasa Indonesia link, record-book lists, "What the quiz covers" (6 topic blocks), "How the quiz is checked" essay, FAQ accordion, then the same 8-column 73-link footer (1,566px tall on phone) ending in a legal disclaimer. **Six store links on one page.**

**The quiz itself (the good part).** Options are tidy; correct pick = green fill + green border; explanation appears as "WHY" with the option repeated ("Correct. They were founded by workers…" — the answer is printed twice, once as the ✓ option and once in the sentence). "Question 2 of 10 · easy" difficulty labels are honest. Explanations are the product's real differentiator and they *feel* like it here — Bergkamp's fear of flying, Vieira's last kick. This is the football.

**Wrong state is under-designed.** On a wrong pick the ✗ option gets only a pink *text* colour (rgb(255,138,130)); no fill, no border change; the correct option gets green *text* only. Compare the correct-pick state, which gets fill + border. So the wrong state — the one that teaches — is the visually weakest state on the page. On a phone in daylight, a colour-only text change on a 14.5px label is the entire feedback.

**Finish screen (after "See your result →").** "Your Arsenal IQ / **46** (50px) / Day Tripper / 2 of 10 · best streak 1 / 2 days in a row (amber)" then four stacked CTAs: green "Keep going — 56 more →" (#quiz), **red** "Play the full Arsenal quiz →" (→ `/play?club=arsenal`), a "Today's Footle" card (→ `/footle`), "Share your Arsenal IQ", then a size chooser "10 Quick · 20 Standard · Full set". 
- "Day Tripper" and "Your Arsenal IQ" are authored, funny, on-brand. Peak of the whole journey. **But** "46" from 2/10 is unexplained — what scale? Riley reads it as a percentage and it isn't.
- **Two "continue" buttons that mean different things.** "Keep going — 56 more" continues *here*; "Play the **full** Arsenal quiz" opens the app. I just played "the Arsenal quiz" — the word "full" tells me what I played was partial, and the button's red (club colour) makes it the loudest object on screen, louder than the green primary. Decision point has 4 CTAs + 3 size chips = 7 choices. Goal 2 and 4: this is where the app funnel is *not* gentle — the hardest sell is the biggest button at the emotional peak.
- "2 days in a row" appeared on a machine that has never finished this quiz before (localStorage `biq.quiz.streak {n:2}`) — a streak claim I cannot account for; either stale state or counting page views. Trust issue for a product whose positioning is "verified".
- Share is a text button with no icon, bottom of stack, transparent — the viral action is the quietest.

**Riley — refresh.** Reloaded after finishing: the page returns to Question 1 with no "you've done today's set — 2/10" state. The *streak* survived ("day 2 ✓" in the card header) but the *result* did not. The site remembers that I came, not what I did. No resume, no done-state, no answer review. State design: done/in-progress states missing on the page that is the product.

**Desktop 1440.** Two-column hero: copy left (H1 541px wide), quiz card right (519px). Reads well and is the best-composed screen on the site — the quiz is above the fold, options readable. But: the two store badges sit *under the H1 blurb, before the user has answered anything* — the first call to action on a page reached from "arsenal quiz" is "leave for the App Store". "More quizzes to try" is a 9-column grid of 116×65 tiles with 3-letter codes (LIV, MUN, MCI…) — a broadcast-graphics convention, nicely football, but the tiles are identical grey and the code is the only differentiator; 24 of them then "Show all 135".
- Hero uses club colour (red ARS badge, red button on finish) — the *one* place on the site where a club's identity is allowed to show. Do more of this.
- Markup leaks the answer: each `<li class="bq-q" data-a="2">` carries the correct index and the explanation is present before answering. Minor, but the SEO copy says "checked twice"; a curious kid with view-source gets 10/10.
- Contrast: "Your Arsenal IQ" label 11px rgb(126,130,140) on #0B0C10 ≈ 3.9:1 at 11px — fails AA for small text. Same muted grey is used for every caption on the site.

## Page 3 — https://balliq.app/play (phone 375 + desktop 1440, both screenshotted)

**This is the fragmentation, measured.** At 375px there are **two sticky headers stacked**: the website header (crest · "Find your club or league" search · hamburger, 57px) and immediately under it the app's tab bar (Play · Daily · Online · Profile · ⚙, 47px). **104px of permanent chrome** before content, two navigation systems with zero overlap (site: Today/Games/Clubs/Quizzes/Lists/Sign in; app: Play/Daily/Online/Profile/Settings) = 11 top-level destinations on one phone screen. Neither system knows the other exists: the site's "Today" anchor and the app's "Daily" tab are the same idea with different names.
- **"Sign in" → `/play`.** On `/play` itself the header's "Sign in" link points at the page you are on. Tapping it reloads. The header DOM contains "Sign in" *twice*. This is the front-door header dropped onto the app without adaptation.
- **Two search boxes 230px apart.** Header: "Find your club or league" (221×40). Page body: "Find your club…" (343×48). Same job, different placeholder, different size, different result surface (one goes to `/quiz/x/`, the other to the in-app club quiz).
- **The page is a second homepage.** Club chips (ARS Arsenal, LIV Liverpool…) = homepage "Your club" again, now with coloured badges the website version lacks. DAILY block (Footle / Daily 7 / Transfer Trail) = homepage "Today" again, now with different card art, a "0/4 today ›" counter in a different style. "More modes" 3×3 grid = homepage "Every game" again with different copy ("Die on wrong", "Quotes & chaos" vs "One wrong answer and it ends"). A first-timer coming from `/` sees the same menu twice in two dialects.
- **Different visual dialect, same palette.** The app's Footle card is a green-glow hero with an "F" logomark, shadowed pill "Play"; Daily 7 is an *amber-tinted* card with an **amber "Play" pill**; Transfer Trail a blue-tinted card with an amber pill. DESIGN.md says the accent is green and spent only on Play — the app spends amber on Play and uses per-mode tints the website never uses. Same hex tokens, different design language: this is exactly why it "looks like a carbon copy of the app".
- **Welcome banner** "Welcome to Ball IQ! Start with today's Footle…" at the top of the app *and* "Good morning / Set your name ✎" *and* an empty profile card "Ball IQ Player · Sunday League · 0 XP / Play a game to get rated / View full profile" *and* "🔥 0 Day streak · Best 0 · LAST 14 DAYS [14 empty boxes] / YOUR FORM: Play a game to see your form." Four empty-state modules for a guest on their first second. Empty states are the loudest thing on the screen; the *game* is below them.
- Desktop 1440: same stack — site header, then app bar, then the app laid out in a 698px + 346px two-column with the profile/streak/form sidebar right. Works structurally, but the "Take Ball IQ everywhere" App Store/Google Play banner (`.home-app-banner`, 698×189) appears *inside the app* — the app tells you to get the app.
- h1 "Home" is 1×1px sr-only; document title "Home — Ball IQ" vs the website's "Ball IQ — Football Quiz…" — different title grammar, another seam.
- Tap targets: "Set your name" 101×19; "0/4 today ›" 65×21; Dismiss × 28×28; Settings tab 26×44; crest 26×26.
- Nothing on `/play` says how to get back to the *website* except the 26px crest. Casey (one-handed) has the crest top-left — the hardest reach on a phone.

# SYNTHESIS

## 1. Design-specificity verdict
**Category-interchangeable shell, product-specific moments.** The system — Inter, #0B0C10 ground, grey cards, one green — is a competent dark-dashboard vocabulary (Linear/Vercel register) that could host a crypto tracker without a token change. The *authored* moments are real and few: the date-and-puzzle-number masthead, the KROOS/RAMOS Footle tiles, the club-quiz explanations ("Non-Flying Dutchman"), "Your Arsenal IQ · Day Tripper", the answer page's voice, the red ARS badge. Where is the football? In the copy, almost never in the design: clubs are three-letter codes on grey; the only club colour on the website is an 8px dot (desktop tiles) and one red badge/button on club pages; the app, oddly, is allowed coloured club chips the website isn't. Verdict: the writing is Ball IQ; the visuals are a template.

## 3. Cognitive load checklist (8)
- **Single focus — FAIL** on `/play` (welcome banner + greeting + "Set your name" + second search + chips + Daily, all above the game) and on the club-page hero (H1 + blurb + store badges + stat strip + quiz).
- **Chunking ≤4 — FAIL**: "Every game" 14 flat rows; club directory 93 rows; footer 71 links in 8 columns.
- **Grouping — PARTIAL**: leagues group clubs well; Today and Every game overlap by 4 items on the same page; `/play` regroups everything again.
- **Hierarchy — FAIL on two screens**: `/play` empty-state modules outrank the game; finish screen's red "Play the full Arsenal quiz" (club colour) outranks the green primary.
- **One thing at a time — FAIL** on `/lists/…` (quiz before the list you searched for); **PASS** on the Footle answer page.
- **≤4 choices at a decision point — FAIL**: finish screen 4 CTAs + 3 size chips = 7; `/play` header+app bar = 11; hamburger 6; homepage Every game 14.
- **No cross-screen recall — FAIL**: site "Today" ≡ app "Daily"; header "Quizzes" lands on a page titled "Football quiz"; the same club goes to the app from a tile and to the site from a chip.
- **Progressive disclosure — PASS** for `<details>` reveal + FAQ accordions; **FAIL** for the 124-row archive and 93-row directory shipped fully open.

## 4. Emotional journey
**Peaks:** the masthead date line ("this is today's paper"); the Footle tile motif; the first explanation after an answer (this is the product's soul and it lands); "Your Arsenal IQ · Day Tripper"; the answer page's "Have a proper go first".
**Valleys:** first second on `/play` — two headers, a welcome banner, "Set your name", 0 XP, 0 streak, 14 empty boxes, "Play a game to see your form" — four reminders that you are nobody before you've done anything; a wrong answer that barely registers visually; the store badges arriving before the first question (desktop hero) and immediately after the quiz card (phone); the finish screen telling you the quiz you just finished wasn't the "full" one; refresh and it's gone.
**The end:** finishing a quiz ends on a good number and a good name, then a red button asks you to leave for the app and a grey one asks you to share; nothing says "come back tomorrow — a fresh order every day" *at the finish*, even though the card header said it at the start. Being asked to sign in: never actually happens — "Sign in" is a mislabelled app link. Landing in the app: you are re-shown the homepage in a second dialect with an app banner inside the app.

## 5. What's working (and why)
1. **The Today block on `/`** — state ("0 of 4 played"), countdown, one accent on one verb, puzzle numbers. It is a daily-paper front page and it is the *only* place the four goals coexist: consistent, navigable, return-inducing, quiet about the app.
2. **Explanations inside the club quiz** — the "WHY" line is the differentiator made visible; the copy has voice and detail; it's the moment a visitor understands why this isn't a content farm.
3. **The Footle answer page** — correct order (hints → reveal → play), spoiler protected with native `<details>`, one primary, two store links only in the footer. It proves the team can do gentle.

## 6. Priority issues
- **[P0] Two shells on one URL.** *What:* `/play` renders the website header (search, Today/Games/Clubs/Quizzes/Lists, "Sign in") *and* the app tab bar (Play/Daily/Online/Profile/⚙) stacked, 104px of chrome, two searches, two nav vocabularies. *Why:* Goal 1 — this is the "carbon copy of the app" screen. *Fix:* one header component with a `mode` prop: on `/play` the site header becomes the app's top bar (crest + search + menu) and the tab bar is the page's nav; rename the app's "Daily" tab to "Today" (or the site's anchor to "Daily") so the two vocabularies merge; remove the body-level "Find your club…" duplicate; make "Sign in" either open auth or read "Open app".
- **[P0] Four multiple-choice widgets.** *What:* `/quiz/<club>/` (`.bq-card`), `/football-quiz/` taster (mono counter + bar), `/lists/…` five questions (numbered prose), and the app's quiz screen are four designs for one interaction. *Why:* Goal 1 — users can't name it but they feel it on every page transition. *Fix:* make `.bq-card` the single web question component (counter, options, WHY, Next) and use it in the taster and the list-page five; while there, give the **wrong state** a fill + border on both the ✗ pick and the revealed ✓, matching the correct state's weight.
- **[P1] One club, two destinations.** *What:* homepage "Your club" tiles → `/play?club=x`; chips/footer/directory → `/quiz/x/`. *Why:* Goals 1 and 2 — the same word takes you to two products. *Fix:* point every club reference on the website at `/quiz/<club>/` (the page Alex calls the product); let *that* page offer the app once, at the end.
- **[P1] The finish screen sells instead of settling.** *What:* red "Play the full Arsenal quiz →" (app) outranks green "Keep going — 56 more"; 7 choices; "46" unexplained; result not persisted; "2 days in a row" on a first visit. *Why:* Goal 3 (the second game) and Goal 4 (gentle). *Fix:* one primary ("Keep going — 56 more"), "Share" as a proper secondary with an icon, a single quiet line "Also in the app — streaks and 1v1 →"; persist the result in localStorage and render a done-state on reload with "Review your 10"; caption the score ("46 — your Arsenal IQ, out of 100"); fix or hide the streak until it's trustworthy.
- **[P2] Store badges everywhere, including the 404.** *What:* 6 store links on a club page (hero + mid + footer), 4 on the 404 above the recovery links, 4 on `/football-quiz/` and `/lists/…`; an app banner *inside* `/play`. *Why:* Goal 4 — presence without pushiness means one, placed after value. *Fix:* delete the hero and 404 badges; keep exactly one "Also on iOS and Android" module per page, positioned after the finish screen / bottom of content; on `/play` remove `.home-app-banner`.
- **[P2] Pages are 7–14 phone screens.** *What:* `/` 6.7, `/quiz/arsenal/` 8.3, `/lists/…` 9.6, `/football-wordle/answer/` 11.3, `/football-quiz/` 13.7 screens; the list table 3.3 screens down; 93-row directory with no finder; 124-row archive open. *Why:* Goal 2. *Fix:* on `/lists/…` put the table first and the quiz after ("Now test it"); collapse archives after 10 rows ("Show all 124"); on `/football-quiz/` add league jump-chips + a filter field at the top of the directory and sticky league headings; on `/` drop "Every game" rows that already appear in Today, and cut the footer to 4 columns.
- **[P3] Put the football back.** *What:* the only club colour on the website is an 8px dot and one red badge. *Why:* Goal 3 — exploring 93 clubs should feel like 93 clubs. *Fix:* the club-colour edge already used on the ARS badge becomes a 4px left rule on every club row/tile and the finish-screen header; champion runs in the PL table get a tinted band; if crests are off-limits, commit to the code-tile system and give it colour everywhere, not only in the app.
