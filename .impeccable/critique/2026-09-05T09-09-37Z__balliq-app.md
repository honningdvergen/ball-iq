## Overall impression

Every page is individually competent and the set does not add up to one website. The header is the one thing that is identical everywhere — same eight links in the same order on every page B measured — and it is the *only* thing: under it, three products begin. `/` is a designed finder; `/quiz/x/` is a static page with its own stylesheet and question widget; `/play` is the app with the website's header bolted on top of its own tab bar. The moments where the product's soul shows (the masthead, the tiles, the WHY, the finish name) are strong enough to carry a site — they are just not connected to each other by anything a visitor can feel.

**Emotional journey.** Peaks: the masthead ("this is today's paper"); the Footle tiles; the first WHY explanation; "Day Tripper"; "Have a proper go first". Valleys: the first second on `/play` — two stacked headers (104px), a welcome banner, "Set your name", 0 XP, 0 streak, 14 empty boxes, "Play a game to see your form" — four reminders you are nobody; a wrong answer that barely registers; store badges before the first question (desktop hero); the finish saying the quiz you just did wasn't the "full" one; refresh and it's gone.
