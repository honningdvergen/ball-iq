## Platform

web

## Users

Today the homepage's traffic is overwhelmingly **first-time visitors with no prior
knowledge of the product** — people who arrived from a search like "Liverpool quiz"
or "football wordle" and have never heard the name Ball IQ. The intended shift,
stated by the owner on 2026-07-31, is toward **returning daily players**.

Measured, not assumed (2026-07-15 cohort read):

- 28 of 58 accounts have never played a single game.
- Activation collapses by cohort: 100% → 38% → 15%.
- A real returning core exists but is small: 8 users have played on 9–22 separate days.
- Footle is the most-played mode. It is invisible in the `scores` table, which
  records only survival/daily/classic/wc2026/chaos/legends — so any claim about
  what people play must not be answered from `scores` alone.

The activation gap, not distribution, is the product's live bottleneck.

## Product Purpose

Football knowledge games. Two things reset every day — **Footle**, a six-guess
surname word game, and the **Daily 7**, seven questions everyone in the world gets
identically so a score is worth arguing about — plus a large library of club,
league, player and tournament quizzes, and real-time multiplayer.

Success is a person coming back tomorrow, not a person reading the homepage.

## Positioning

Three things a neighbouring quiz product could not truthfully copy:

1. **The bank is verified inline, to a zero-error bar.** 6,407 questions, each put
   through generate → examiner → adversarial skeptic, with only double-survivors
   committed. Competing football quizzes are scraped or generated unchecked.
2. **Most answers explain themselves.** 5,186 of 6,407 questions (80.9%) carry a
   written explanation shown after answering. NOT all of them — the claim "every
   answer is explained" has already shipped once as false and was corrected. Per
   category: Premier League 100%, Transfers 100%, Champions League 100%, History
   96%, Ligue 1 95%, Legends 84%, Records 82%, Managers 79%, La Liga 74%, UCL 74%,
   Serie A 68%, Bundesliga 64%, Euros 56%, World Cup 53%.
3. **Small clubs get the same depth as giants.** 72 club packs; Hajduk Split is
   treated with the same care as Real Madrid. This is an editorial commitment, and
   it is the reason the club pages out-hold the app itself (109–145s vs 122s).

## Operating Context

- **66% of sessions are mobile.** The homepage is read on a phone, one-handed.
- **Search is the discovery route.** Club and player pages rank and bring people in
  cold; the head term "football quiz" sits around position 41.
- Measured behaviour: pages that are **playable** hold 109–145s. Static lists
  without anything to play got 2.3s. Playable beats readable, with evidence.
- Scroll depth on the long club pages stops around 25%.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React + Vite PWA; Capacitor shell for iOS and Android; Supabase backend; Vercel
  hosting. **`main` IS production and auto-deploys on push.**
- `src/App.jsx` is a ~10k-line monolith. `src/marketing/MarketingHome.jsx` owns the
  marketing homepage. 191 SEO pages are generated by `scripts/gen-seo-pages.mjs`.
- **The web build ships inside the native binary** (`webDir: "dist"`), so anything
  added to the web surface must be native-guarded or it rides along. The native
  apps' store listing and privacy policy declare **no ads and no analytics**; the
  web surface carries AdSense. Detect native on both platforms — the `capacitor:`
  protocol check catches iOS only.
- Content is free and stays free. Paid tier, if it happens, is features and
  cosmetics, never questions.
- Build is gated: ESLint, a distractor-plausibility audit, and a rule that any
  club/category SEO page needs ≥15 explanation-bearing questions or the build fails.

## Brand Commitments

**None are binding.** Asked directly on 2026-07-31 what must survive a redesign, the
owner answered "nothing is binding — replace it all", explicitly putting the name
lockup, the green, and the ball mascot on the table.

**Never put the exact question count on a surface.** Stated by the owner on
2026-07-31 and previously: "6,407 questions" reads as dumb — a spec-sheet number
that invites a competitor to print a bigger one, and that means nothing to a
person who has not played. The size of the bank may be *felt* (breadth of clubs,
"the other ones are waiting") but not *counted*. Same for "5,186 explained": the
coverage figure is an internal truth for honesty checks, never a marketing line.
This binds copy, not the record — the measured figures stay in this file.

One factual consequence to carry forward, not a constraint: the current green
(`#58CC02`) and Inter are used across the app UI and all 191 generated pages. Scope
for this round is **homepage first, then decide**, so a replacement world will be
temporarily inconsistent with those surfaces. That was accepted knowingly.

## Product Principles

1. **Playable beats readable.** The measured difference is 109–145s versus 2.3s.
   Anything above the fold that cannot be played is spending the budget badly.
2. **Never claim more than the bank can prove.** "Every answer explained" was false
   at 80.9% and had to be retracted. Numbers on any surface get measured first.
3. **Activation is the bottleneck.** Half of all accounts have never played. A
   change that wins attention but not a first play has not worked.
4. **The obscure club is the moat.** Depth on Hajduk Split is the thing competitors
   will not do, and it is what makes the search strategy defensible.
5. **One person builds this.** Prefer things that stay true without maintenance over
   things that go stale silently.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA is the target. Known live failures as of 2026-07-31, from a deterministic
scan of the homepage: four instances of body text at **2.9:1** (`#5b6070` on
`#14161c`, needs 4.5:1), two blocks of uppercase body text at 119 characters, line
length running to ~98 characters, and line-height as tight as 1.08. Touch targets
must hold 44×44 given the 66% mobile share.
