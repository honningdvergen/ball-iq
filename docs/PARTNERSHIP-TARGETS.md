# Ball IQ — Partnership & Outreach Target List
**Compiled 2026-09-01. Verdicts below come from live-web verification passes run 2026-08-31/09-01; the FotMob sitemap sweep and the Sports Mole contact decode were re-run by me in this task (see §5).**

---

## 1. State of play — read this before writing a single email

**The FotMob × fcQuiz mechanic is DEAD, and the pitch currently cites it as live.** I re-verified this myself in this task, not from the handoff: I pulled all 33 FotMob EN news sitemaps and parsed them. They contain **exactly 32 URLs matching "quiz", the newest published 2024-04-27**, while the same sitemaps are current to **2026-08-30** — i.e. FotMob has published zero quizzes in 28 months, on a site that publishes daily. The collaboration itself was real (two plain dofollow links per article, syndicated across ~20 locale paths, hub still live at fcquiz.app) but it was **five quizzes across three weeks in April 2024**, not a perpetual weekly engine. Anyone Alex pitches can find the same dead hub in ninety seconds, so the sentence "FotMob runs a weekly quiz built free by an outside partner" must be rewritten as "FotMob ran one in 2024, it worked, and nobody is running it now — the slot is open." Beyond that, the second finding of this whole exercise is that **most big score-aggregators do not link out at all**: Flashscore (six articles, three domains, zero external editorial links), BeSoccer (won't even link Fabrizio Romano when quoting him), Bola.net (hyperlinks "Fabrizio Romano" to its *own* tag page), Placar de Futebol (no articles exist — 6,095 machine-generated URLs) and Sofascore (two articles enumerated, zero third-party links) all fail the only test that matters. What survived is a tier down: **mid-size and single-club editorial sites that demonstrably emit dofollow credit links in body copy, several of which have a dead quiz franchise of their own to revive.** The honest expected value of this campaign is therefore not "a dozen linking URLs a week from a top-tier domain, forever" — it is roughly one dofollow link per week per publisher, from domains of unmeasured authority, from a shortlist of thirteen. That is still worth doing, but it should be resourced as one weekly build reused across several publishers, not as bespoke content per partner.

## 3. Bespoke pitch angle — top 5, one line each

1. **The Peoples Person** — "You already run a free third-party widget on every article and credit it with a link; I'm offering the same deal one level up: a weekly Manchester United quiz, hand-written and fact-checked, built and run by me, published under your byline, for the same credit link EasyPolls gets."
2. **Grand Old Team** — "Your `/everton/quiz/` tag is wired and empty and your last quiz was 2017 — I'll fill it every week for free, in the same recurring-fan-format style as your annual Fan Survey; you keep the content and the audience, I take one text credit link in the body." *(Insist on a text link — their 2017 precedent was a Sporcle iframe with a prose name-check and no anchor, which passes nothing.)*
3. **The Celtic Star** — "I'm not asking you to start something new — I want to restart the Friday Quiz you ran in 2018 and David Potter's 20-question era quizzes, weekly, free, forever, and you never touch it." *(Lead with the dormant franchise; do not say the word "link" in the opening paragraph — the parent network sells link inventory.)*
4. **The Football Faithful** — "Your Friday Quiz and Monday Quiz died on 12 February 2021 when the Sporcle embeds stopped; I'll revive that exact slot every week with hand-written fact-checked questions instead of user-generated ones, free, in exchange for a dofollow text credit — not an embed."
5. **Sports Mole** — "You run an entire Off the Pitch section for lighter fan content and have zero quizzes or games anywhere on the site; I'll fill that hole with a free weekly Premier League quiz, and the credit link is already your own house style — you dofollow-credit Sport Witness and TEAMtalk by name in body copy every day."

**One term that must appear in all five emails:** the credit is *a dofollow text link in the article body, not an iframe embed*. Three of the five targets' only prior precedent (Sporcle at The Football Faithful, Sporcle at Grand Old Team, fcQuiz at The Blizzard) was an embed that passed zero equity. If that term is raised after the content starts flowing, it will lose.

## 4. DO NOT SEND

**Killed on the link test — they emit no external editorial links, so there is nothing to be paid in:**
- **Flashscore / Livesport** — six articles across three domains, 2023–2026, zero external editorial links; their own ad page states "We don't sell text links". Also writes quizzes in-house, so a "you have no quiz" opener is factually wrong.
- **BeSoccer** — names Romano, Sky, Marca, Mundo Deportivo, The Athletic in a live blog and hyperlinks none of them. Separately, they are a paid B2B data/content supplier: our offer *is* their product, sold for money.
- **Bola.net** — links "Fabrizio Romano" to its own tag page; names BBC and City AM as sources and links neither. Policy, not oversight.
- **Placar de Futebol** — no articles exist. All 6,095 sitemap URLs are machine-generated; /noticias, /blog, /artigos all 404. Only outbound links are undisclosed Amazon/Shopee affiliate tags.
- **Nutmeg Magazine** — exactly one hand-authored outbound editorial link in a ten-year, 32-article sample, and it points at a YouTube Short. Quarterly print magazine, ~5 web pieces per issue, mostly paywall teasers.

**Killed because they already have the product:**
- **Football League World** (Valnet) — shipped an in-house interactive quiz engine with score persistence and per-quiz telemetry in April–May 2026.
- **365Scores** — 10+ in-house quizzes 2025–2026 under their own interns' bylines, including a World Cup 2026 group-stage quiz on 28/06/2026.
- **Mackolik** — has already built clones of **all three** Ball IQ modes in-house (Bul Beni! = Footle, Ben Kimim? = Mystery Player, Kariyer Yolu = Career Trail), plus a multiplayer knowledge grid.
- **Balls.ie** — ran a football-first weekly office quiz in Feb 2026 and deliberately replaced it with a broader mixed-topic format; the slot is sponsor-monetised and owned by a named staff writer.
- **Forza Football** — built, subscription-gated and then retired their own Live Quiz; v7.0 shipped a Prediction Game; the free daily editorial slot is held by Sports Illustrated.

**Killed on brand safety / link quality — the link would be worth little and sit in bad company:**
- **World Football Index** — 7 undisclosed dofollow links to offshore casinos across 26 recent posts (27%), while correctly nofollowing their own NewsNow widget. They know how; they chose not to.
- **LiveScore Group** — licensed gambling operator (LiveScore Bet, Virgin Bet); free games are sportsbook acquisition funnels. Wrong neighbourhood for a store-rated football app, and their news is licensed PA/Stats Perform wire, so there is no editorial desk to pitch.

**Killed as structurally impossible:**
- **These Football Times** — published nothing since 06 April 2023; operating company dissolved 17 June 2025 (Companies House 14701914); support@ is currently the address customers are using to chase undelivered magazines.
- **The Football Ramble** — has no website. footballramble.com 301s to a domain that fails TLS and dead-ends on an unrelated retailer's 404. Also makes its own "ULTIMATE … Quiz" series on YouTube.
- **FotMob** — do not pitch. They ran this exact deal and walked away inside a month; pitching them re-opens a decision they already made.

## 5. What I could not verify — gaps, stated plainly

**Verified by me directly in this task (2026-09-01):** the FotMob quiz sweep (33 sitemaps, 32 quiz URLs, latest publication date 2024-04-27, sitemaps current to 2026-08-30); the Sports Mole contact address, re-decoded from the live page's Cloudflare tokens rather than trusted from the handoff; the presence of "Partnerships"/"Collaborations" on the live Sofascore corporate contact page; and that the fcQuiz FotMob hub still returns HTTP 200.

**Everything else in this report is inherited from the verification passes dated 2026-08-31/09-01 and was NOT independently re-fetched by me in this synthesis step.** Those passes recorded raw-HTML anchor and `rel` enumeration, which is the right method; but a claim is only as fresh as its fetch, and sites change.

Specific unresolved gaps:

- **No authority metric exists for any target on this list.** Ahrefs, Similarweb and Moz were unauthenticated in every pass. There is no DR, no traffic figure, no referring-domain count for a single one of the thirteen. The ranking is therefore ordered by *observed linking behaviour and likelihood of a yes*, not by measured link value. Sports Mole is ranked 5th on the assumption it is the largest domain in the qualified set — that assumption is unmeasured.
- **The Blizzard has two contradictory verdicts on file** (SEND from the link-out pass; DEAD from an earlier adversarial pass). Both are factually correct about the same evidence — they *do* pay dofollow credit to an external quiz partner, and that partner *is* fcQuiz. I have ranked it 12 rather than resolving the conflict, because the disagreement is a judgment call about competitive positioning, not a factual dispute. Alex should make that call.
- **Sofascore's dormant in-app "Sofa Quiz"** may still exist buried in the current mobile app; the store description is not an exhaustive feature list and nobody installed the app. Likewise, whether Sofascore has *ever* granted an outbound dofollow link is unproven — the sample was two articles.
- **Contact-inbox monitoring is unverified everywhere.** No form was submitted and no mail was sent. Deliverability for The Celtic Star is inferred from live MX records only. The Football Faithful's commercial@moveup.media is JS-rendered and could not be confirmed by curl this pass. The Blizzard's info@ address does not appear in raw HTML at all.
- **Two targets have a paid-link footprint that may cause the pitch to be priced rather than accepted** — Online Gooner (undisclosed dofollow casino links in Arsenal articles) and Bluemoon-MCFC (a "Guest writer" slot dominated by gambling destinations). No rate card is published on either site, so "the guest slot is sold" is a strong inference from the link pattern, not a confirmed fact. It also means their outbound equity may be discounted by Google.
- **Nobody was identified by name at any target, and nobody should be.** Every route above is public and role-based.
- **Quiz-absence findings rest on nav, sitemap and site: search.** Several targets' internal search was broken or JS-gated (Arseblog, Grand Old Team). "No quiz product" is well-evidenced but is an absence-of-evidence claim in each case.
- **Whether any of this converts is entirely unmeasured.** Per the standing lesson that shipping is not delivering: thirteen sends producing thirteen dofollow links from unmeasured mid-tier domains has no established relationship to moving "football quiz" off position 41. Treat this list as a cheap experiment with a defined weekly cost, and instrument the outcome before scaling it.
