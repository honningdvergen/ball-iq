02What actually happened since Tuesday

The work was real. Footle's keyboard now claims every pixel, verified by twelve hand taps including the screen-edge column. Colour-blind Footle works. The Play listing rewrite is live with a proper feature graphic. Hard questions lacking an explanation went from 155 to 4. Streak repair, the 30-day back-catalogue and Android FCM all shipped and were verified in production.

And not one outcome number moved. iOS ratings went 2 → 3, with the US storefront at zero. Play still shows no stars at "50+ downloads". k-factor was 0.23 then and is 0.23 now. Friendships formed in thirty days: zero, from fifteen warm opponent pairs. Web push went from zero subscribers to one, and the subscriber is you.

Five gameplay gaps, four store gaps and four retention items closed in 48 hours. None of it moved a number, because almost none of it reached anyone.

03Three things no single reviewer saw

These came out of the synthesis reading all fourteen reviews together. Each one is worse than anything in an individual report.

Your CI has been lying for thirty runs

continue-on-error:true sits on the e2e job in ci.yml, so GitHub reports success while the job fails. It has failed on all thirty consecutive runs — two deterministic failures across all three WebKit projects. The only automated check on your conversion path has been off, behind a green tick.

The club pages are now 39% of everything played

club_quiz_results holds 208 plays in the last 7 days against 210 in the last 30 — essentially all club-page play happened this week, against 324 total score rows. Clarity shows 420 club-page sessions a week versus 10 on /lists. The moat is already converting, on the one surface with a 0.11–0.20 field CLS, colour-only grading, no aria-live and no instrumentation. It is the busiest game in the product and the least finished.

Question QA is switched off, and a wrong answer is live

question_review's newest row is dated 9 June. Roughly 600 questions have shipped since, at a historical 12.5% rejection rate. One is now provably wrong in the shipped bundle: Atlético's capacity is keyed at 68,000 when the Metropolitano has held 70,460 since 2023 — and 72,000 is one of the four options, so the bank marks the closest answer wrong.

05Stop doing

A plan with no stop-list is a wish list. The panel emitted roughly 45 quick wins and 38 five-star moves for one person with no income — this is the half that says no.

  • Stop shipping new game modes. Mystery Player has 6 plays from 4 players all-time, against a 5M-visit incumbent that owns the format. Stadiums shipped with zero telemetry.
  • Stop polishing the store listings. The craft is top-decile and it is not the constraint — the app is absent from the top 25 for "football quiz", "football trivia", and even "footle", the name of its own mode.
  • Stop publishing localised listings while the binary is English-only. There is no i18n anywhere in src/, and none of the ten drafted descriptions says so.
  • Stop tuning the rating prompt. Three ratings is a denominator problem. Worse, the widened web ask points at stores where most of those asked structurally cannot rate.
  • Stop re-auditing SEO fundamentals. Settled and recorded; the ceiling is authority. Three reviewers re-derived the same numbers this round.
  • Stop cutting native builds from memory. preflight-release.mjs is a good gate that is in no script, no CI, and no checklist.

06Where the panel disagreed with itself

The critic named /lists the single biggest lever — "the only surface where Ball IQ has hundreds of humans a week instead of dozens." The synthesis went and measured it: 10 Clarity sessions a week on /lists against 420 on the club pages. The critic was reasoning from impressions; the synthesis was counting humans.

I am flagging this rather than hiding it, because it is the most useful thing the exercise produced. Both were looking at real data. Only one was looking at people.

07The edge, and how to press it

6,694 questions, 84% carrying a written explanation, across 86 club packs. The direct competitors advertise 500–1,000 questions and not one of them ships explanations at all. Difficulty labels validate almost perfectly against real play — easy 89.7% correct, medium 69.2%, hard 50.3%.

That is not a feature a funded rival copies in a sprint. It is thousands of hours of hand-written football prose, and it is why /play holds 92–94% scroll depth.

Press it in this order: make the club-page taster work properly for everyone, keep it fresh while the season is young, deepen the seventeen thin packs so the surface does not run dry after two visits, then instrument it — so the next panel argues about measurements instead of about which surface matters.
