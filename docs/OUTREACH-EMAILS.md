# The five outreach emails — ready to send

Written 2026-09-01, off the back of `docs/PARTNERSHIP-TARGETS.md`.

**Send these from Alex's own inbox, not from balliq.app.** Two reasons, both
matter:

1. **Deliverability.** balliq.app sends product email — the win-back and
   activate campaigns, day-2 emails, streak reminders. Cold outreach attracts
   spam complaints, and complaints against that domain would damage delivery of
   the mail actual users need.
2. **Reply rate.** A solo developer in Norway writing personally gets answered.
   A brand address does not. The story *is* the pitch.

## Rules baked into every draft

- **Never write the words "dofollow", "backlink", "SEO" or "link building".**
  To a fanzine editor those read as an SEO spammer, and two of these targets
  sit under networks that sell link inventory. The ask is phrased as a normal
  human credit: *"a normal text link back to the site in the article."*
- **The credit term is stated in the FIRST email, never raised later.** Three of
  these five have prior quiz precedent that was an embedded iframe passing zero
  value. If we let the content start flowing and then ask, we lose.
- **No question count anywhere.** House rule; also invites scrutiny that adds
  nothing to the pitch.
- **Ask for a reply, not a meeting.** Lower cost to say yes.
- Short. Editors are busy and a long cold email reads as a sales sequence.

## If someone says yes

The whole economic case rests on **one quiz built per week, reused across every
publisher** — not bespoke content per partner. Club-specific targets (1, 2, 3)
need a club-flavoured variant; the general ones (4, 5) can share a single
Premier League set. If that stops being true, the cost outruns the return and
this should be stopped.

**Instrument it before scaling.** Per the standing lesson that shipping is not
delivering: thirteen links from domains of unmeasured authority have no proven
relationship to moving "football quiz" off position 41. Track whether a live
link actually moves a position before committing to a weekly cadence forever.
