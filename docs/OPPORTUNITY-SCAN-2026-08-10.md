# Opportunity scan — 2026-08-10 (fresh-eyes, six lenses)

43 findings from six lenses (first-visit UX, game modes, growth loops, language/copy, retention, competitors), merged and cross-checked against docs/TODO.md.

## Executive read — three themes that recur across lenses

1. **Finished work is invisible.** The Scouting Report homepage isn't what crawlers see; Trail and Mystery have no web pages; the lineup builder sits inert; five viral loops fire zero analytics events. Four lenses independently found "built but not surfaced/measured" — the leak is at the last step, again.
2. **Playable beats readable, and the winners win on mechanics, not MCQ variants.** Gamemodes and competitors converged on the same three gaps: grid mode, guess-the-XI, and type-in recall over /lists — all proven formats, all buildable from data Ball IQ already owns.
3. **The distribution bottleneck has cheap unpulled levers.** Every share surface is generic or ghost-anonymous; the one loop reachable by non-users (SEO-page share) has no challenge in it; the FotMob→fcQuiz partnership existence proof lands exactly on board item #1.

## The three bets I would make before PL kickoff

Given: the bottleneck is signup volume, the season starts in under two weeks, and the lineup builder is nearly ready.

1. **Send the partnership pitch this week.** It is already board #1, it is the only lever that multiplies signups rather than adding them, and the FotMob→fcQuiz proof plus season timing make the pitch strongest right now. Alex-owned; everything I can do (pitch material, verified-bank credentials) is done. Pair it with the split-brain homepage fix so the page any partner checks is the page users see.
2. **Make the /lists surface playable.** It is the largest measured leak (half the site's impressions, near-zero clicks), the fix is a proven decade-old format over data already published, and it converts season-spike browsers into players instead of bounces. Instrument the share loops in the same push so the season's traffic teaches us which loop to feed next.
3. **Ship the guess-the-XI daily from the lineup builder — with Alex's sign-off.** The expensive half exists and is currently inert behind the competitor-parity bar; a Missing-11-style daily is a different, smaller promise the assets already clear, it gives the builder work a payoff before the window closes, and it lands a third daily plus its own landing page exactly when football attention peaks. If Alex holds the bar, substitute the Higher-or-Lower fee mode: smallest effort on the board for a genuinely new mechanic.

The grid mode is the best build after these — flagged independently by two lenses as the genre's strongest share artifact, but it is L-effort with answer-key-class data work, and it should not compete with the season window.
