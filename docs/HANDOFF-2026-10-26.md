## 1. The one thing that matters: Google traffic collapsed on 20 October

What happened. Google clicks fell from about 130 a day to about 10 a day overnight on 20 October, and impressions from about 4,400 to about 100. Still at that level on 24 October, the last day Search Console had data for. Brand searches (ball iq, balliq) still work. Pages that do appear keep their old position (the Arsenal page sat at 6.6 before and 7 to 8 after) but are shown almost never. Bing was unaffected. No manual action, no security issue, nothing broken. That shape is a site-level quality or spam filter, not de-indexing and not a ranking slide.

What caused it, as far as four independent audits and a build-to-build comparison can tell. Between 14 and 20 October we did, within about 24 hours on 15 and 16 October:
- put 109 near-duplicate "questions and answers" pages back into the index (about 45% of each repeated its club page word for word);
- published 29 new templated club pages and 20 Top 10 lists;
- added about 8,800 non-breaking hyphens and 1,800 invisible zero-width characters into the question text on 406 of 471 pages (a fix to stop scores wrapping; Google reads that as hidden text, and it broke matching for searches like 1988-89);
- the pages also carried Google's Education Q&A markup on football trivia (a guideline misuse, since July) with an invisible "soccer quiz" alternate name, and a sitemap that stamped the build date on every URL every deploy.
Ruled out with evidence: cloaking, bot blocking, redirects, host health, Safe Browsing, blocklists, scrapers, negative-SEO links, an announced Google update, structured-data errors, orphan pages.

## 8. Gotchas that bit us this week

- Vercel builds run the whole vitest suite; a slow or wall-clock test breaks production deploys silently (a red deploy leaves prod on the previous build). Always `npm run build` locally before pushing; always watch the deploy run and check /version.json after.
- The build ends with the Home JS budget gate (867 of 909 KB today). New modes must be lazy.
- Daily schedules are FROZEN (Footle, Daily 7, Mystery, Trail, Top 10, grid): never regenerate or reorder served days. The grid is unreleased, so its schedule may be regenerated once in v2.
- tests/unit/shell-i18n.test.js forbids claiming questions are "written by hand" or "checked by hand"; say "researched against sources and verified".
- A question is retired by tagging it (tag:"unverified" or tag:"duplicate"), never deleted; ids stay stable. Translated rows (scripts/seo/clubs-*.mjs) that point at a retired id must be removed or the build fails.
- The app's name on anything public is "Ball IQ: Football Quiz". First name only in the byline.
- Cloud sessions: never also press `run` after run_once_at (it starts a duplicate); clear connectors right after create; cloud sessions cannot be messaged from the Mac, only from claude.ai.
- Never use the Manifold Search Console connector for balliq.app; read GSC in Alex's Chrome.
