# Guess the Club by Emojis — proposal + curated puzzle bank

Alex's worry (2026-08-20): "I don't know enough clubs that have emojis that
link them." The research answer: **pure nickname-emoji coverage alone is thin
(~25 strong clubs), but mixing three puzzle types gets to 60+ fair puzzles**
— and the third type scales indefinitely because we author it.

## The three puzzle types

**Type A — Nickname/crest (the fair ones).** The emoji IS the club's
established identity: crest animal, nickname, or universally-known symbol.
These feel like recognition, not decoding. ~25 exist in England alone.

**Type B — Colours + one anchor.** Two colour circles plus one distinguishing
emoji. Weaker alone, good as easier filler and for clubs with no animal.

**Type C — Rebus/pun (the funny ones, infinite supply).** Emojis that SOUND
OUT or pun the club name, like the viral "guess the film from emojis"
format. These are hand-authored jokes — the quality ceiling is high and the
supply is unlimited, which solves the coverage problem completely.

## Format recommendation

**A level pack, not a daily.** 8 puzzles per round, three rounds (Kickoff /
Cup Run / Extra Time) climbing in difficulty; multiple-choice from 4 clubs on
tap (typing club names is friction the joke doesn't need). If it lands and we
ever want a daily variant, the frozen-answer-log law applies from day one.

Hint ladder mirrors Stadiums: tap = reveal the club's league, tap again =
first letter. Same event/XP plumbing (`biq:emoji-completed`), same
Sporcle-adjacent share line ("🧩 8/8 — guessed them all from the emojis").
