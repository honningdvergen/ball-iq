### 1. `q_5461f9` — STALE

**Category:** Currency-risk overlay  
**Question:** _"The record fee paid for an English player is £105m — who was sold for this fee in 2023?"_  
**Keyed answer:** Declan Rice ✓ *(answer was correct at time of writing)*  
**Issue:** The question uses present-tense framing ("the record fee … **is** £105m") and its hint states the fee "edged past the £100m Man City paid for Jack Grealish in 2021." By July 2026 the record has been broken twice:

- Elliot Anderson (Nottingham Forest → Manchester City) — reported **£116m**
- Morgan Rogers (Aston Villa → Chelsea) — reported **£117m**

Both players are English. Rice's £105m is no longer the English-player transfer record as of this question's "as of 2026" context.

**Recommended fix:** Reword as a historical question: _"In 2023, which player became the most expensive English player of all time at £105m?"_ Answer and all options unchanged.

**Sources:** Sky Sports / IANA (Rice July 2023); Times Live / Goal.com (Rogers to Chelsea July 2026, £117m); NST (Anderson to Man City July 2026, £116m).

### 2. `q_c00ab2` — FACTUAL_ERROR (hint)

**Category:** Rotating slice (index 1)  
**Question:** _"When Wayne Rooney scored his iconic overhead kick in the 2011 Manchester derby at 78 minutes, what was the score at that moment?"_  
**Keyed answer:** 1-1 ✓ *(confirmed correct — Nani 41', Dzeko/Silva deflection 65', Rooney 78' for the winner)*  
**Issue:** The hint reads _"Rooney's bicycle kick **broke the deadlock** and was later voted the best goal in Premier League history."_ In football commentary "broke the deadlock" means scored from 0-0 to 1-0. Rooney's goal took the score from **1-1 to 2-1** — it did not break a 0-0. The hint directly contradicts the correct 1-1 answer and will mislead players into choosing 0-0.

The "voted best PL goal" clause is accurate (won the Premier League's 20th-anniversary fan poll, 2012).

**Recommended fix:** Replace hint with:  
_"Rooney's bicycle kick put United 2-1 ahead — turning a 65th-minute equaliser — and was later voted the best goal in Premier League history in a 2012 fan poll."_

**Sources:** Man City official match report (2011-02-12); Fox News Flashback Friday match recap; ITV/Premier League goal poll (2012).

### 3. `q_be16bb` — FACTUAL_ERROR (hint)

**Category:** Currency-risk overlay  
**Question:** _"Cristiano Ronaldo scored how many goals across all his time at Manchester United in both spells?"_  
**Options:** 103 / 118 / 123 / **145**  
**Keyed answer:** 145 ✓ *(confirmed — 118 goals in first spell, 27 in second, per Man United official exit statement)*  
**Issue:** The hint reads _"118 in his first spell (2003-09) plus 27 in his second **(2021-22)** makes 145."_ Ronaldo's second spell ran from August 2021 to **22 November 2022** (contract terminated). He played in both the 2021-22 and 2022-23 seasons, scoring approximately 24 goals in 2021-22 and 3 more in 2022-23 before the termination. Labelling all 27 second-spell goals as "(2021-22)" is factually wrong and will mislead players who know he was at United into autumn 2022.

**Recommended fix:** Change the parenthetical to _"27 in his second spell (2021–22 and into November 2022)"_.

**Sources:** Man United official statement on Ronaldo exit (22 November 2022); Planet Football / 90min (24 goals all competitions in 2021-22); The Score (contract termination date confirmed).
