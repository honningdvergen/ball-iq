**Issue:** The year "2016" is wrong. Bojan Krkić joined Stoke City on a four-year permanent deal in **July 2014** (after a loan that began in January 2014). The "2016" date does not correspond to any Bojan–Stoke transaction; by 2016 he was out on loan to other clubs.

**Recommended fix:** Change "2016" to "2014" in the question stem.
> "Which Spain international, a Champions League winner, joined Stoke City in **2014**?"

### 2. q_9a324e — WRONG_ANSWER

**Question:** "Which Welsh former Liverpool striker managed Swansea City to **four promotions** from 1978?"
**Options:** John Toshack / Ian Rush / Mark Hughes / Dean Saunders
**Keyed answer:** John Toshack (index 0)
**Hint:** "Toshack took them from the Fourth Division to the First in four seasons as player-manager."

**Issue:** The hint (correctly) says **four seasons**, but the question says **four promotions** — these are different claims. Going from Division 4 to Division 1 requires exactly **three** promotions: 4th→3rd (1978–79), 3rd→2nd (1979–80), 2nd→1st (1980–81). There is no fourth promotion.

**Source:** SwanseaCity.com club history; FourFourTwo; footyanalyst.com ("3 promotions in 4 years").

**Recommended fix:** Change "four promotions" to "three promotions" in the question stem.
> "Which Welsh former Liverpool striker managed Swansea City to **three promotions** from 1978?"
